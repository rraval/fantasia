Lyric is the starting city on the Material Plane where the campaign began, site of major riots and the transposition event that swapped parts of it with wilderness.

## Description

- Major city on Material Plane
- Had first judicial office with [[Vadu]] and [[Ura]]
- Site of [[Material Plane Riots]]
- Parts of city literally transposed with other locations
- Now overrun by jungle after transposition
- Has [[Tinkers Guild]]

## Districts/Locations

- First judicial office (courthouse with "LAW ONE" sign)
- North section (most fires during riots)
- East and west (less fires)
- South (eerily quiet during riots)
- Lord's manor (near southern edge, close to Fey)
- [[Tinkers Guild]]

## Chronology

### The Riots Begin

- City on fire due to [[Material Plane Riots]]
- Party members tried and sentenced in courthouse
- [[The Leader]] interrupted trial with minions
- North had most fires, east/west less, south eerily quiet
- Orphanage on fire - party rescued children including [[Drummer Boy]]
- Deserted houses in southern section
- Lord's manor had single humanoid silhouette
- [[Helia Oriela]] sensed land particularly close to Fey near manor
- Party fled south through city gates under martial law

### Transposition Event

- [[Feywild Riots]] occurred 2 full moons before [[Material Plane Riots]]
- [[Transposition Event]] occurred the morning after the party escaped Lyric
- Party witnessed vines shooting from the ground, city collapsing and being overrun by wilderness
- Commoners quoted "For What It's Worth" right before transposition occurred
- ~1500 humans from Lyric transported to [[Feywild]]
- Wilderness transported to replace Lyric sections
- [[Tinkers Guild]] experiment with [[Instrument Panel]] caused transposition

### Aftermath

- Parts of Lyric literally transposed into [[Feywild]] [[Town of Diamonds]]
- Appeared in patches throughout the town
- Humans were corralled by military, performing labor

### Return

- Party used [[Knife of Transposition]] to return ~1500 humans
- Multiple portal openings to transport everyone back
- [[Ura]] found trying to escape, learned [[Vadu]] was dead
- Ura tried to lead Lyric after riots

### Post-Transposition

- City overrun by jungle
- Wilderness replaced urban sections
- Power structure collapsed with [[Vadu]]'s death
- [[Kool]] referenced as "Lyrical gangster"

## Connections

- Connected to [[Ode]] (mining town nearby)
- Linked to Feywild through transposition
- [[The Grove]] (halfling settlement in region)
