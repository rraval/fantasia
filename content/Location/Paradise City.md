Paradise City is a major city on the Material Plane with a train station, home to [[Tensegrity]] (the floating fortress of [[Piano Man]]).

## Description

- Major city with train station
- [[Tensegrity]] hovers above, suspended by strings
- Shows signs of decay: blemishes, cracks, buildings falling apart
- Many creatures huddled around garbage fires
- Revolution brewing among water elementals and citizens

## Key Locations

- Train station (connects to [[Ode]] region via "City Boy" train)
- [[Tensegrity]] - floating fortress above the city
- Various inns where party stayed
- Uptown district

## Chronology

### Arrival by Train

- Party traveled from [[Ode]] region via train
- Train called "City Boy" (Small Town Girl was in repairs)
- Arrived after fighting off [[The Synth]]'s assassination attempt

### Journey to Tensegrity

- Party escorted by [[Grondar Blackfloyd]] toward [[Tensegrity]]
- Noticed city in disrepair as they passed through:
  - Blemishes and cracks visible
  - Buildings falling apart
  - Many creatures huddled around garbage fires
- Arrived at [[Tensegrity]], massive structure with spikes and strings

### Revolution Brewing

- Water elementals gathered at inn to behold [[Cory|Corminar]]
- Recognized him as "Tide Turner, Champion of the Undertow"
- [[Lucius]] joined party here
- [[The Synth]] assassins ambushed the inn:
  - Mist creature turned into werebear
  - Yochlol strumming theremin attacked

### Post-Coup

- Train revolution was happening
- Party jumped out window with [[Grondar Blackfloyd]] and [[Ragnar Rok]]
- Pass without trace used to escape
- Revolution tied to water elementals and [[Cory]]'s emergence

## Current State

- [[Piano Man]]'s reign ended when he stabbed himself with [[Knife of Transposition]] to foil [[The Coup]]
- [[Uptown Girl]] and [[Amras]] retreated to Uptown after failed coup attempt
- [[Vadu]] confirmed dead
- Revolution in progress

## Connections

- Train connection to [[Ode]]/[[Lyric]] region
- Site of [[Tensegrity]] fortress
- Home to revolutionary factions
- Water elemental population significant
- City deteriorating as strings' power wanes
