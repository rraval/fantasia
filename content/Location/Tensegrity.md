Tensegrity is the floating fortress of [[Piano Man|Man of Strings]], a massive structure suspended by strings with presence in every plane.

## Description

- Massive structure with spikes sticking out of ground
- Suspended entirely by strings
- Giant floating islands with structures upon them
- Metallic beams jutting out
- Has elevator system
- Chamber with giant piano
- Presence in every plane simultaneously

## Structure

- Ground level with elevator entrance
- Multiple floating islands/levels
- Upper chamber where Piano Man resides
- Uptown district (where [[Uptown Girl]] and [[Amras]] retreated)

## Chronology

### History

- Established by [[Piano Man]] after overthrowing [[Belle]]
- Singular stronghold with presence in every plane
- Symbol of age of reason and diplomacy
- Central to Piano Man's multi-planar governance

### Party's Visit

- Party escorted by [[Grondar Blackfloyd]]
- Approached massive structure
- [[Amras]] waited at bottom of elevator entrance holding [[Tonic]]
- [[Uptown Girl]] operated elevator
- Ascended to Piano Man's chamber
- Chamber had giant piano where Piano Man sat
- [[Augur of Melodies]] delivered prophecy here
- Augur died after proclaiming "answer is blowin' in the wind"
- Piano Man granted party banner to compete in [[The Intervals]]
- Party stayed at luxurious inn using banner's authority

### Post-Coup

- [[The Coup|Coup attempt]] failed when [[Piano Man]] stabbed himself with [[Knife of Transposition]]
- Piano Man intentionally trapped his own soul to deny conspirators their prize
- [[Uptown Girl]] and [[Amras]] retreated to Uptown section
- Denying [[Elyas]]' involvement in coup

### Collapse

- After the party escaped, Tensegrity collapsed
- The floating fortress fell and its wards were broken
- Desertification began spreading through the area
- [[Uptown Girl]] took control of the ruins
- The collapse marked the definitive end of Piano Man's age of Strings

## Significance

- Physical manifestation of Piano Man's power
- Exists across all planes simultaneously
- Engineering marvel suspended entirely by strings
- Symbol of Strings' dominance over other forces
