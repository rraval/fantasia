The Roly Poly Little Bat Faced Girl is a seedy pub in the [[City of Hearts]], whose barkeeper Al sent the party to a dangerous occultist shop.

## Description

- Seedy pub in [[City of Hearts]]
- Run by Al the barkeeper
- Frequented by less reputable clientele
- Place people "don't come back from" when given bad directions

## Staff

- Al - barkeeper (later revealed as neutral aligned despite sending party to danger)

## Chronology

### Al's Interrogation

- [[Archer]] fetched Al at [[Queen of Hearts]]' request
- Party needed evil soul for demonstration
- Al was bound and brought to Queen
- [[Harloc]] insulted, beat, healed, and threatened him
- Detect Good and Evil revealed Al was neutral (not evil)
- Al admitted: "you were annoying, I wanted you gone, and people that go there don't come back"
- Referring to the occultist shop he'd directed party to
- Al was released as not suitable for soul harvesting
