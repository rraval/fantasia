The Tinkers Guild is an organization in [[Lyric]] that built the [[Instrument Panel]] and conducted the experiment causing the [[Transposition Event]].

## Description

- Guild of inventors/tinkerers in [[Lyric]]
- Experimented with musical instruments and planar magic
- Built complex musical contraptions
- Responsible for transposition event

## Activities

- Experimentation with musical transposition
- Built [[Instrument Panel]] (lyre, drums, pan flutes)
- Combined musical instruments with gems and lenses
- Created planar rifts/tears

## The Experiment

- Conducted experiment with [[Instrument Panel]]
- Combined musical instruments, gems, and lenses
- Successfully created transposition between planes
- Swapped ~1500 humans from [[Lyric]] with wilderness
- Created [[Planar Rip]] weakening between planes
- Time differential: Feywild time moves 2x faster

## Connections

- [[Viscount Bridgerton]] had [[Pan Flute]] and a note connecting him with [[The Synth]]
- [[Knife of Transposition]] uses similar principle (gems + souls)
- [[Maurice]] and other powers aware of guild's work
