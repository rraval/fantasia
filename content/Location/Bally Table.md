Bally Table is a magical pinball machine dungeon at the top of a mountain where [[Stodor]] (stone giant) was chained and [[Bob]] (wizard) was trapped.

## Description

- Magical pinball machine functioning as dungeon
- Located at top of mountain
- Trapped/enslaved creatures within
- Operated as actual pinball game mechanics

## Inhabitants

- [[Stodor]] - stone giant chained to the machine
- [[Bob]] - wizard trapped inside
- Operated by [[Viscount Bridgerton]] in a mech suit
