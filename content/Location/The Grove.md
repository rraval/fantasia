The Grove is a halfling settlement near [[Ode]] where [[David Blane]]'s wedding took place.

## Description

- Halfling settlement
- Community hosting weddings
- Near [[Ode]] and [[Lyric]] region
- Subject to goblin raids

## Chronology

### David's Wedding

- Party brought [[David Blane]] here for his wedding
- David's three brothers present
- This was David's third attempt at wedding
- Wedding ceremony began
- Main hobbit officiant asked for objections
- Cart overturned revealing 2 goblins and goblin boss
- Goblins tried to scare halflings
- Party fought goblins:
  - Cast entangle and sleep spells
  - Knocked out 2 goblins
  - 2 more goblins appeared
  - Goblins hustled David away to lair
- Party negotiated with goblin boss for info
- After rescuing David, party returned for shotgun wedding
- [[Cory]] officiated the wedding
- Party received [[The Blane Map]] as reward
- Party warned halflings about potential goblin attack

## Inhabitants

- Halfling community
- David Blane's family including three brothers
- David's bride
- Various halfling guests and officiants
