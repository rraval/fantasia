Wise Men Falls is a waterfall where [[Jack]] was murdered and his spirit remained trapped, part of the tragic love story that created the [[Dress of Love]].

## Description

- A waterfall with a lake at the base
- The site where [[DeWitt]] killed [[Jack]]
- Became haunted after Jack's death
- The waterfall stopped flowing after Jack's murder

## History

Wise Men Falls was the site of [[Jack]]'s murder and haunting:

**The Murder:**
- [[Princess of Diamonds|Bonnie]], consumed by jealousy, orchestrated a deadly trap
- She lied to Jack, telling him [[Rose]] would meet him at the falls
- She lied to [[DeWitt]], telling him Jack was a vampire
- DeWitt ambushed Jack at Wise Men Falls
- Killed him with a crossbow bolt through the heart
- Jack's body fell into the lake

**The Haunting:**
- After his death, Jack's spirit remained at the falls
- The waterfall mysteriously stopped flowing
- The lake became blocked with Jack's body trapped at the bottom
- Jack's spirit haunted the misty falls
- Tried to convince visitors to jump into the lake
- Unwilling to move on without [[Rose]]
- Required DEX saves to avoid falling in

**Resolution:**
- The party eventually retrieved Jack's body from the lake bottom
- With the body recovered, the lake unblocked
- The waterfall activated again, the mist lifted
- Jack's spirit was finally able to move on after reuniting with Rose's spirit
- This allowed Rose to find peace and create the [[Dress of Love]]
