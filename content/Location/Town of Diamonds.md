Town of Diamonds is a fae town in the Feywild that was transposed with parts of [[Lyric]], becoming the holding location for ~1500 humans before they were returned home.

## Description

- Fae town ruled by House of Diamonds
- Parts of [[Lyric]] literally transposed into it
- Large military presence after transposition
- Secret tunnel system
- Princess of Diamonds' mansion (actually Green Hag)

## Key Locations

- Princess/Town mansion with [[Dress of Love]]
- Military messenger tent
- Secret tunnel entrance
- Corralled areas for human laborers

## Chronology

### Session 7 - Discovery

- Party approached with [[Princess of Diamonds]] (Green Hag in disguise)
- Saw remains of [[Lyric]] transposed in patches throughout town
- Large military presence controlling humans
- Humans corralled into dedicated areas, performing labor
- [[Helia Oriela]] stopped at door trying to sneak in (disguise as Harengon didn't work)
- Helia retconned secret passage into town
- Emerged next to messenger tent
- Questioned messenger who revealed:
  - Town seized under Ace of Spades' orders
  - Queen of Hearts as acting emissary
  - No one seen Ace of Spades since humans arrived
  - Princess of Diamonds "not well regarded, that creature deserved what came to her"

### Session 8 - Dress Heist

- Party disguised and returned to steal [[Dress of Love]]
- Used secret tunnel to enter
- Mansion break-in:
  - [[Louis Orrai]] put guard to sleep
  - [[Harloc]] paralyzed another
  - Party rushed in under one minute
  - Fought rug of smothering trapping [[Helia Oriela]]
  - Killed 2 pixies
  - Found [[Moon Orb]] in desk's false bottom
  - Materialized [[Dress of Love]] with orb
- [[Archer]] led troops pursuing them
- Party triggered traps, killed 2 scouts
- Escaped using pass without trace

### Session 13 - Human Transport

- Party returned with [[Queen of Hearts]]' sealed writ
- Found people of [[Lyric]] being held
- Used writ to access commander
- Created portal tears to transport ~1500 humans:
  - First tear: ~200 people (started trample, left valuables)
  - Second tear: ~400 women with children (easier to maintain reused portal)
  - Third tear: ~600 people
  - Fourth tear: remaining people
- Guards found [[Ura]] trying to escape
- Ura learned [[Vadu]] was dead, hung head in shame

## Governance

- Nominally ruled by Princess of Diamonds (actually Green Hag)
- Seized by Ace of Spades' forces after transposition
- [[Queen of Hearts]] acting as emissary
- Ace of Spades hasn't been seen since humans arrived
