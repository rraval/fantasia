City of Hearts is the fae city in the Feywild ruled by [[Queen of Hearts]], site of the ball massacre and party's base during Feywild arc.

## Description

- Major fae city in Feywild
- Queen of Hearts' seat of power
- Has mansion with library
- Contains seedy pub called [[The Roly Poly Little Bat Faced Girl]]
- Subject to red cap violence during riots

## Key Locations

- Queen's mansion (with library that was destroyed)
- [[The Roly Poly Little Bat Faced Girl]] pub
- Ball venue (where [[8 of Spades]] and [[Vadu]] were killed)
- Servant quarters

## Chronology

### Background

- [[Feywild Riots]] occurred two full moons before [[Material Plane Riots]]
- Red caps organized and became dominant political force
- Coincident with Material Riots, Ace of Spades disappeared with [[Chords of Power|chord of power]]
- [[Lyric]] [[Transposition Event]] occurred (corresponding to Material Plane riots)

### Party Base

- Party operated from City of Hearts
- Investigated occultist shop with [[Planar Rip]]
- [[Princess of Diamonds]] (Green Hag) claimed Queen seized control
- Party ultimately worked with Queen

### The Ball

- Queen hosted ball with "LIVE AND LET LIVE" theme
- [[Prince of Darkness]] accompanied Queen (she was influenced)
- [[8 of Spades]] gave toast, was decapitated by red cap
- Queen brought [[Vadu]] to ball
- Red caps massacred attendees
- [[Vadu]] killed
- Queen fell unconscious
- Prince of Darkness killed
- Party fought red caps and reflections

### Recovery

- Party investigated Prince of Darkness body (exploded in sunlight)
- Queen woke, met with party and [[Archer]]
- Discussed transposition crisis
- Party demonstrated [[Knife of Transposition]] portals
- Discovered 2x time differential (Feywild faster than Material)
- Queen provided resources for human transport

### Experimentation

- [[Hanzo]] returned to Feywild via [[Maurice]]
- Party took over library to experiment with reflections
- [[Helia Oriela]] accidentally destroyed library with thunderwave

### Bargain

- Party bargained with Queen for supplies:
  - Helia: banner of Queen of Hearts
  - Harloc: one large black arrow
  - Hanzo: wagon
  - Party: sealed writ of authority

## Connections

- Connected to [[Town of Diamonds]] where humans were held
- Part of larger fae political structure (Hearts, Spades, Diamonds, Clubs)
- Linked to Material Plane through transposition events
