The Love Shack is a cottage where [[Rose]]'s ghost waited for [[Jack]], part of the tragic love story that created the [[Dress of Love]].

## Description

- A cottage near the seamstress workshop
- Haunted by [[Rose]]'s spirit
- Site of Rose's vigil waiting for Jack
- Connected to the water mill where the Dress of Love was eventually created

## History

The Love Shack became the center of [[Rose]]'s ghostly existence after [[Jack]]'s murder:

**Rose's Haunting:**
- After Jack was killed at [[Wise Men Falls]], Rose died of grief
- Her spirit remained at the Love Shack, unable to find peace
- She examined a locket, holding onto memories of Jack
- Asked visitors if they were Jack
- When disappointed, she would scream and cry
- In her anguish, summoned [[DeWitt]] (as a boneclaw) and a death dog

**Finding Peace:**
- When the party recovered Jack's body from [[Wise Men Falls]]
- They told Rose what truly happened to Jack
- Rose finally understood the truth of [[Princess of Diamonds|Bonnie]]'s deception
- She found peace and left the Love Shack
- Returned to the seamstress cottage where the [[Dress of Love]] was created

## Connections

- Part of the [[Dress of Love]] origin story
- Where [[Rose]] waited as a ghost
- Near the seamstress cottage with the water mill
- Contrasts with [[Wise Men Falls]] where [[Jack]]'s spirit waited
