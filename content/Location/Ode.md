Ode is a mining town near [[Lyric]] on the Material Plane, with connections to the [[Cult of Darkness]] mine and [[Viscount Bridgerton]].

## Description

- Mining town
- Near [[Lyric]]
- Has inn called [[The Lone Beatle]]
- Dwarven mining operation with train station
- Southwest road leads from Lyric

## Key Locations

- [[The Lone Beatle]] inn
- [[Cult of Darkness]] mine (nearby)
- Mining regions

## Chronology

**First Visit:**
- Party traveled southwest from [[Lyric]] toward Ode
- [[Hanzo]] delivered orphan children to [[Sam]] here
- Hanzo initially wanted to leave party after delivery
- [[Louis Orrai]] joined party near Ode (poorly hiding in bushes)
- Party looped east through forest to avoid going through village proper

**David Blane Incident:**
- Party discussed heading toward city but not through Ode
- Encountered [[David Blane]] east of Ode in forest
- [[The Grove]] (halfling settlement) nearby

**Mine Discovery:**
- [[Sam]] revealed [[Viscount Bridgerton]]'s dealings
- Viscount handed children to [[Cult of Darkness]] mine near Ode
- Party traveled to small mine
- Found horrific torture of children
- Encountered [[Drummer Boy]] as undead entity

**Mine Exploration**
- Party continued exploring cult mine
- Encountered [[Prince of Darkness]] mage
- Found remains of cult priests killed by [[Drummer Boy]]

**Train Station**
- Ended up at dwarven mining operation (actually train station)
- Dwarf clerk validated invitation
- "Small Town Girl" train in repairs
- Took "City Boy" train to [[Paradise City]]
- [[Ragnar Rok]] and party dealt with Hungry Sorrowsworn
- Found +1 weapons at station

## Connections

- Southwest of [[Lyric]]
- Near [[The Grove]] (halfling settlement)
- [[Cult of Darkness]] mine in vicinity
- East through forest connects to wider region
