- Narrative resumes in the back of [[Maurice]]'s wagon
- [[Louis Orrai|Louis]] notices the [[Knife of Transposition|dagger]] glowing and examines it
- The party gets sucked into the dagger
- Find themselves in a very small translucent cavern with foggy edges, can see the caravan through the walls
- Hear a solitary figure, half the [[Piano Man]], playing a [[Tumbi]]: [[Yesterday|"Yesterday... all my troubles were so far away"]]
- [[Helia Oriela|Helia]] throws a fireball at the figure, which accidentally destroys the [[Tumbi]]
- The figure whips around and is upset at the party, asking them to fix this only instrument
- [[Helia Oriela|Helia]] attempts to mend the instrument but the magic is too strong to restore the string
- [[Velden]] possesses some knowledge of the [[Tumbi]] but not enough to effectively repair the instrument
	- 2 songs that involve Tumbis
		- [[Mundiyan to bach ke]]
		- [[Get your freak on]]
- This half of the [[Piano Man]] gives up on his instrument and attempts to convince the party to restore him back to power, to be his champions in earnest
- Party is unconvinced. [[Piano Man]] dismisses them, pointing at a cylindrical elevator and wishing them luck getting out
- Party examines elevator, sees one small button (down) and one large button (double down)
- Party guesses double down and a voice speaks "major". They go past a desert-like level full of scorpions, and down one further level
- They emerge in a similarly sized room that is otherwise empty. There is another identical elevator across from them. Pushing the single down button does nothing
- Investigating the area finds symbols representing "2 of 7"
- The party decides to go double down again
- They go past a forest level full of baddies
- They emerge again in an empty room. Investigating finds "3 of 7" and arrows: down labelled "Shadowfell" and up labelled "Material"
- The party goes single down
- They emerge in another empty room. They mess with the elevator a bit
- [[Velden]] manages to rotate the single down button. Pressing it now opens the ceiling back to the previous empty room, but the elevator is otherwise stationary
- The party seems convinced there's no way back to the Material plane
- They deduce that these elevators are following a major scale and very quickly navigate the remaining whole-whole-whole-half sequence. They fly past lovingly crafted levels full of interesting monsters and encounters
- They emerge with the last elevator embedded into the ground
	- They are in a mountainous region with the sky and ambient light in a permanent state of twilight
	- They see a spire off to the distance, smelling of brimstone
	- Behind them, there is a massive clocktower that they ignore
- As they trek towards the spire, the wind picks up
- They realize that in [[Shadowfell]], the wind whistles, in the same tune as [[There It Go]] (the whistle song)
