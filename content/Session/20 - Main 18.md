Mage guides party to a suspiciously flat plane ([[Bally Table]]) at the top of a mountainous region. There is a single rotating cylindrical structure with metallic tracks and 3 massive balls at the top.

The party needs to solve a smell identification puzzle (with a real cocktail smoke kit). There is a furnace with an small etched icon. Each correct solve loads a ball into the machine.

With the third ball loaded, the cylinder rotates to reveal an elevator. Going down starts a pinball machine sequence [[Pinball Wizard]]
- Party starts at top of machine. They step out, leaving the Mage behind.
- Ball locks release monsters: Clockwerk Scout, Ballista, ...
- Tilt that opens up lava river
- Multiball
- Giant that throws balls out [[Stodor]]
- Destroy 3 balls to power down the machine
- Discover that bumpers are actually jails holding [[Bob]] and a warrior. Opening one jail causes the other to be incinerated.
- Party drops into ball return to end up near the giant.
