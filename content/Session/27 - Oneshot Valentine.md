# The Drama

Go so seamstress cottage
Run down, water mill, loom inside.
Meet Bonnie.

Go to Love Shack.
Meet Rose. Examining a locket.
She asks if the party is Jack.
When she realizes it's not Jack, she screams and starts crying.

Exclaims "my father that monster", summons DeWitt and dog.
DeWitt has seamstress shears in his throat.
DeWitt also uses two cranking cross bows.
On death, DeWitt is a talking head.

At Wise Men falls, Misty DEX saves to avoid falling.
The spirit of Jack tries to convince the party to jump into the lake.
The body is stuck at the bottom.
The body has a crossbow through its heart.
The party has to get the body out (the spirit is unwilling to move on without Rose)

With the lake unblocked, the water fall activates again.
The mist lifts, Rose is back at the cottage.
The party tells Rose what happened to Jack.
She is at peace, possesses the water wheel.
Her heart strings feed the loom.
And one bolt of cloth is produced.

Bonnie appears quickly, takes moonlight cloth.
She reveals that she loved Jack first, but love turned to jealousy.
She lied to DeWitt convincing him that Jack was a vampire.
She lied to Jack, saying that Rose would meet him at the falls.
