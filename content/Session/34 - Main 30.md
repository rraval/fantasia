- Following the party's chess boxing victory, the handsome devil and the leaders of the remaining 29 of the [[36 Chambers]] begrudgingly kneel to [[Flo|Master Flo]] and the party
- [[Flo|Master Flo]] announces a grand celebration that evening to honour the new champions
- The party suspects the devil is connected to the bone devil last seen with the stolen instruments from [[Lyric]]
	- They try to question him and are told to find him later, during the party
	- The devil shows a brief reaction at the mention of [[The Synth]]

# The Celebration

- [[Helia Oriela|Helia]] sets off fireworks as the party gathers around a large fire
- The party talks briefly with [[Flo|Master Flo]]
	- Each of the [[36 Chambers]] is a different remnant of the [[War Against Hippity-Hops]]
	- The "war" is a misnomer; many species were genocided
	- Hip-hop was a global phenomenon
- At the devil's tent (surrounded by lesser demon and devil minions):
	- [[Helia Oriela|Helia]] tries the [[Dress of Love]]
		- It has no effect on the devil himself, but begins to work on his minions
		- The devil cleaves through his own minions in response
		- He wears armor and giant horns, with a massive sword strapped to his back and a glowing whip
	- The party accuses the devil of being part of [[The Synth]]
		- He reveals himself as [[The Synth|the Fifth Prophet]], leader of the Synth
		- "Pleased to meet you, hope you guessed my name"
		- The party deduces this is _the_ devil
	- The party debates whether to fight _the_ devil and, in a narrow vote, decides not to

# The Clocktower Trap

- Later, atop the broken clocktower, the devil is laughing at the party for believing they had bested him, claiming he is not allowed to lie
	- He casts Sanctuary on himself as the party begins to regroup and retreat
	- He raises a hand and slams a shutter shut, insisting the party cannot leave because they still "have business"
- As they talk, the whistling wind of the [[Shadowfell]] ([[There It Go]]) grows louder and assembles a spoken warning drawn from [[Against the Wind]]
	- Fragments build up: "see the run," then "Janie was lovely," then "watch the young man run"
	- At full frenzy it becomes clear: "watch the young man running against the wind"
	- Nature and Arcana checks confirm the wind is deliberately trying to warn the party of something bad
- The devil volunteers information about the stolen instruments from [[Lyric]]
	- He admits to conducting experiments with them, apologizing for the trouble with the lyrics
	- Asked his goal, he claims to be trying to "establish power over the musical gods"
	- In a truth-for-a-truth exchange, he reveals he has used the instruments to "understand the truths of the universe and augment various parts of my army"
- The devil offers a deal: he will sell the party the betrayal of a "certain friend," in exchange for their loyalty and a favor performed at the moment of the betrayal
	- The party deduces the "friend" is [[Maurice]], the establisher of the cycle
	- [[Louis Orrai|Louis]] worries [[Maurice]] "cannot die," making betrayal pointless, but the party notes a betrayal need not involve killing
	- The party does not accept the deal
- [[Louis Orrai|Louis]] looks out and sees a massive battle below: the 29 opposing clans against hundreds of demons
	- The party realizes the entire clocktower conversation was a trap to stall them while the battle raged
	- Three struggling banners bearing [[Flo|Master Flo]]'s colors are visible in the chaos

# The Devil Is a Decoy

- The devil takes out his glowing whip and offers a "performance," attacking [[Harloc]] as the party turns to leave
	- The whip restrains [[Harloc]] for 6 damage, reducing his speed to zero and granting advantage on attacks against him
	- [[Harloc]] cuts the magic whip for 9 damage, but the whip is unaffected; combat begins in earnest
- [[Helia Oriela|Helia]] casts Moon Beam on the devil for 18 damage (failed Constitution save)
	- Moonbeam instantly reverts any shapechanger to its true form
	- The devil's skin ripples, literally separates from the body, and runs off the platform
	- Left behind is a smaller, mechanically-enhanced humanoid construct, not a devil; the "devil" was a decoy the whole time
	- The construct still grips the whip, keeping [[Harloc]] restrained
- [[Louis Orrai|Louis]] casts Shatter on the inorganic construct for 18 damage (failed save at disadvantage)
	- The construct begins speaking in a much more robotic voice

# Escape and Charge

- The party escapes by leaping off the tower, with [[Louis Orrai|Louis]] casting Feather Fall to land the whole group safely
	- [[Harloc]] grapples the construct (athletics 25 vs. the construct's 5), mounts his steed, and drags it off the tower with the party
	- They land in the battle roughly 200 feet from [[Flo|Master Flo]]'s embattled banner
- [[Helia Oriela|Helia]] shifts into her warhorse form; [[Louis Orrai|Louis]] casts Haste on the horse (double speed, +2 AC, advantage on Dexterity saves, extra action)
- The party charges through the battle, forming a "double horse clothesline" with the whip, dealing up to 46 force damage to anything in its path
	- They cover the first 120 feet through groups of devils and become the center of attention
	- Near a Goliath-like creature, the construct's whip-arm gives way, freeing [[Harloc]], who claims the magic whip for himself

# The Wind of Change

- Approaching the banner, the party hears a chorus carried on [[Flo|Master Flo]]'s voice, turning the tide of his battle
	- The chorus ([[Wind of Change]]) sings of "the wind of change" and "children of tomorrow," and the music's pitch suddenly drops
	- This creates a 40-foot bubble of slowed time around the banner, causing enemies to retreat
	- A History check reveals "children of tomorrow" refers to the orphanage of orphans in [[Lyric]] - the children the party rescued during the [[Material Plane Riots]] - tying the song to them
	- The effect reads as a manifestation of the coming [[Forces#Wind|Wind age]] of change
- New threats close on the bubble: two cannon-and-helm constructs and a very large spidery construct
- The party enters the slowed-time bubble on horseback, advancing only about 10 feet
	- Outside the bubble, the constructs move twice as fast as the party
	- The unfolding spider deals 27 force damage; the party is at a disadvantage inside the bubble
	- An Arcana check reveals the slow-time effect is an external area spell cast by the large spider, not a ritual from the banner
	- The party realizes it needs to get out

# Defeating the Spider

- As the party exits, the machines stop targeting them and turn on the allies still inside the bubble
- [[Harloc]] grabs the devil construct to throw back into the circle as a distraction; its chest piece comes off, then he successfully hurls it toward the banner
	- The construct initiates a self-destruct sequence (a common feature of these mechs)
- [[Louis Orrai|Louis]] loses concentration on Haste after taking damage (Constitution save failed)
- The spider fires a beam at [[Flo|Master Flo]], freezing him in place; cannon fire follows, one shot hitting [[Harloc]] for exactly his armor class
- [[Helia Oriela|Helia]] (still a warhorse) re-enters the bubble to kick the self-destructing construct away from the allies
	- Her first kick fails, only clipping the head; [[Louis Orrai|Louis]] uses his Portent to retcon the roll into a success
	- The kick sends the construct bomb soaring out of the bubble
- The party disengages and runs, anticipating the blast
	- The construct explodes in a massive shockwave, dealing 10 damage to everyone inside the bubble
	- The explosion ends the combat: the spider is destroyed, the other constructs are wrecked, and the two flying constructs retreat

# Aftermath

- [[Flo|Master Flo]] remains paralyzed amid barely-coherent, unconscious, and dead allies; [[Louis Orrai|Louis]] treats him with a medicine check and Flo begins moving again after "36 seconds"
- [[Harloc]] mends the whip's physical damage, but its magic is not restored; the mended sections are noticeably less restraining
- The party salvages two intact legs from the dead spider construct, identifying them as powerful lances usable as weapons
- Investigating the wrecked machine, they find a tiny trapdoor and retrieve a set of [[Pan Flute|pan pipes]] hidden inside
- Retrieving the pan pipes triggers a surprise self-destruct: a gigantic fireball at least 30 feet wide
	- [[Velden]] sprints past the group at full speed, the "young man running against the wind" the wind's [[Against the Wind|warning]] had foretold
	- The party, caught on the edge of the 40-foot blast radius, takes damage as the session ends

# Recovered

- The devil's whip, physically mended but with its magic (and much of its restraining property) lost
- Two spider-construct legs, salvaged as powerful lances
- A set of [[Pan Flute|pan pipes]] pulled from the wrecked construct
