The Orchestral Plane appears as a giant orchestra hall representing the cosmic structure of reality.

## Appearance

- Giant orchestra hall layout
- Strings section closest to front
- Piano to the left
- Winds (woodwinds and brass) top central
- Percussion top right
- Conductor's section (pile of bodies)

## Characteristics

- Dream-like plane accessed through visions
- Represents cosmic order and structure
- Shows the three [[Forces]] in their places
- Each visitor has ethereal bindings extending to different sections

## Sections

### Strings

Located closest to the front of the hall, the strings section appears intact and functional.

**Inhabitants:**
- [[Piano Man|Man of Strings]] avatar - apparition with incoherent ramblings and no memory of recent events
- Woman violin avatar
- Giant double bass avatar

### Percussion

Located in the top right of the hall, the percussion section shows signs of severe damage.

**Condition:**
- Contains ripped portals labeled 2, 3, 4, 5 out of 7 total subsections
- 4 subsections are in shambles
- Wind chimes present that react to disturbances

### Winds

Located in the top central area, comprising woodwinds and brass instruments.

**Condition:**
- Pristine and undamaged
- Appears fully functional

### Conductor Section

The conductor's area contains a disturbing pile of goblin bodies.

**Features:**
- Large pile of bodies with various written notes
- Notes recovered include ominous messages: "DIE DIE DIE" and "What does the fox say"

## Chronology

### Dream Vision During [[The Coup]]

After [[Piano Man]] stabbed himself with the [[Knife of Transposition]], the party experienced a shared vision of the Orchestral Plane while resting in [[Tensegrity]].

**Discoveries:**
- Each PC had 3 ethereal bindings: 2 strong and 1 weak
- First strong binding led to percussion section
- Second strong binding led to conductor section
- Weak binding led to strings section

**Actions:**
- Party showed the [[Knife of Transposition|dagger]] to the strings section avatars
- Investigated percussion section's ripped portals
- Examined the pristine winds section
- Recovered notes from the conductor's pile of bodies

The vision was interrupted when guards knocked on their door with news of [[Vadu]]'s death.

## Connections

- Represents the cosmic structure binding [[Belle]] and the [[Cantum cycle]]
- Connected to the three [[Forces]]: Percussion (drums of war), Strings (order and diplomacy), Winds (change and transformation)
- [[Maurice]] revealed connection to the conductor section through his bindings
- Part of the [[Planar System|seven-plane system]]
