The multiverse of Fantasia consists of multiple interconnected planes of existence, each with unique properties. Seven planes were involved in the binding of [[Belle]], though not all have been fully explored.

## Material

**The Prime Material Plane**

The "normal" reality, home to [[Lyric]], [[Paradise City]], and other major locations.

### Characteristics

- Standard time flow (baseline for comparison)
- Home to humans, dwarves, orcs, and other common races
- Contains [[Lyric]], [[Paradise City]], [[Ode]], [[The Grove]]
- Subject to [[Material Plane Riots]]
- Partially swapped with Feywild in [[Transposition Event]]

### Key Locations

- **[[Lyric]]** - Starting city, site of riots and transposition
- **[[Paradise City]]** - Major city with [[Tensegrity]], showing decay
- **[[Ode]]** - Mining town with [[Cult of Darkness]] mine
- **[[The Grove]]** - Halfling settlement
- Train stations connecting regions

### Time Flow

- Baseline (1x normal)
- Moves half as fast as Feywild
- When viewed from Feywild through portal: voices lower pitched, movement appears slowed

## Feywild

**The Realm of Faerie**

Parallel plane of wild magic, fae creatures, and natural chaos, home to [[Queen of Hearts]] and fae nobility.

### Characteristics

- **Time moves 2x faster than Material Plane**
- Home to fae, satyrs, pixies, red caps, hags
- Wild magic and nature-based power
- Political structure based on card suits (Hearts, Spades, Diamonds, Clubs)
- More chaotic and magical than Material Plane

### Key Locations

- **[[City of Hearts]]** - Queen of Hearts' seat of power
- **[[Town of Diamonds]]** - Where transposed humans were held
- Green Hag's ancestral cave (former [[Princess of Diamonds]]'s lair)
- The Roly Poly Little Bat Faced Girl pub
- Rope bridge ravine where red caps were trapped

### Political Structure

**The Four Houses:**
- **Hearts** - [[Queen of Hearts]], currently in power
- **Spades** - Ace disappeared with [[Chords of Power|chord]], status unknown
- **Diamonds** - "Princess" was actually Green Hag, house in squalor
- **Clubs** - In disrepair after events centuries ago

### Time Differential

- 2x faster than Material Plane
- 2 moons pass in Feywild = 1 moon on Material

### Inhabitants

- [[Queen of Hearts]] and fae nobility
- [[Archer]] - captain of guard
- Red caps - violent fae
- Satyrs, pixies, quicklings, darklings
- Green hags, dryads, other fae creatures

## Orchestral

**The Orchestral Plane / Dream Plane**

Appears as a giant orchestra hall, representing the cosmic structure of reality.

### Appearance

- Giant orchestra hall layout
- Strings section closest to front
- Piano to the left
- Winds top central
- Percussion top right
- Conductor's section (pile of bodies)

### Characteristics

- Dream-like plane accessed through visions
- Represents cosmic order and structure
- Shows the three [[Forces]] in their places

## Shadowfell

**The Plane of Shadow**

Dark reflection of Material Plane, briefly mentioned in campaign.

### Known Information

- Bone devil fled to Shadowfell during combat
- Exists as darker parallel to Material Plane
- Has jungle region around Lyric area
- Not directly explored by party

## Elemental Plane

**Realm of Pure Elements**

Mentioned as plane "above" Feywild in cosmic hierarchy.

### Known Information

- [[Helia Oriela]] mentioned it's thought to be next plane up from Feywild
- Presumably home to elementals
- Water elementals on Material Plane may have connections
- Not directly explored by party

## Other Planes

The binding involved seven planes total, but not all have been identified or explored:

1. Unknown
2. Shadowfell
3. Material Plane
4. Feywild
5. Unknown
6. Unknown
7. Unknown

The Elemental and Orchestral planes are confirmed to exist, but it is unclear where they stand in the hierarchy.

Each plane received a [[Chords of Power|Chord of Power]] after [[Belle]]'s defeat.

## Planar Travel

### [[Transposition Event]]

- [[Tinkers Guild]] [[instrument panel]] swapped populations
- ~1500 humans from [[Lyric]] to Feywild
- Wilderness to Material Plane
- Created [[Planar Rip]] weakening

### [[Knife of Transposition]]

- [[Maurice]]'s dagger opens controlled portals
- Requires soul gems for power
- Can create tears between any planes
- Time differential visible through portals

### Natural Connections

- [[Planar Rip]]s allow passage between planes (see also [[Knife of Transposition]] and [[Instrument Panel]])
- [[Tensegrity]] exists in every plane simultaneously
