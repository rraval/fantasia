The Realm of Faerie is a parallel plane of wild magic, fae creatures, and natural chaos, home to [[Queen of Hearts]] and fae nobility.

## Characteristics

- **Time moves 2x faster than [[Material Plane]]**
- Home to fae, satyrs, pixies, red caps, hags
- Wild magic and nature-based power
- Political structure based on card suits (Hearts, Spades, Diamonds, Clubs)
- More chaotic and magical than [[Material Plane]]

## Key Locations

- **[[City of Hearts]]** - [[Queen of Hearts]]' seat of power
- **[[Town of Diamonds]]** - Where transposed humans were held
- Green Hag's ancestral cave (former [[Princess of Diamonds]]'s lair)
- The Roly Poly Little Bat Faced Girl pub
- Rope bridge ravine where red caps were trapped

## Political Structure

### The Four Houses

- **Hearts** - [[Queen of Hearts]], currently in power
- **Spades** - Ace disappeared with [[Chords of Power|chord]], status unknown
- **Diamonds** - "Princess" was actually Green Hag, house in squalor
- **Clubs** - In disrepair after events centuries ago

## Time Differential

- 2x faster than [[Material Plane]]
- 2 moons pass in Feywild = 1 moon on Material
- When viewing [[Material Plane]] through portal: voices higher pitched, movement appears accelerated

## Inhabitants

- [[Queen of Hearts]] and fae nobility
- [[Archer]] - captain of guard
- Red caps - violent fae
- Satyrs, pixies, quicklings, darklings
- Green hags, dryads, other fae creatures

## Chronology

### [[Transposition Event]]

- ~1500 humans from [[Lyric]] transported to Feywild
- Held in [[Town of Diamonds]]
- Wilderness swapped to [[Material Plane]]

## Connections

- Part of [[Planar System|seven-plane system]] involved in [[Belle]]'s binding
- Received a [[Chords of Power|Chord of Power]] after [[Belle]]'s defeat (held by Ace of Spades)
- Connected to [[Material Plane]] through [[Transposition Event]]
