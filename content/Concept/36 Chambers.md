The 36 Chambers are faction remnants of the [[War Against Hippity-Hops]] that occurred hundreds of years ago. Each chamber has towns, cities, and villages scattered across the [[Shadowfell]], maintaining their distinct identities and territories.

## Organization

Each of the 36 chambers maintains its own settlements and population throughout the Shadowfell. When significant political matters require discussion, delegates from each chamber gather (most recently at a Gothic Baroque castle built into a mountainside).

During such meetings, each chamber establishes a temporary camp in a circle around the meeting point. The 36 distinct camps display different banners representing their chambers. Notable banners observed include one with a gun-shaped emblem and another that was bloody red.

## Political Landscape

The chambers are divided between two major factions:

**[[Flo|Master Flo]]'s Faction:**
- Consolidated seven chambers under their leadership
- Seeks to "rise up and restore the honor that was stolen"
- Advocates for reviving the militant traditions of the hip-hop heritage

**Opposition Faction:**
- Mixed composition: harengons, dark elves, dwarves, bone creatures, and devils
- Led by a handsome devil as main speaker
- Argues for living in peace rather than seeking war
- Maintains control of the remaining chambers

## The Mystery

The chambers follow an ancient hip-hop tradition called "the mystery" - a binding battle system where disputes are resolved through champion combat. When Master Flo invoked this tradition at a castle meeting, the opposition accepted and declared the challenge would be [[Da Mystery of Chess Boxin|chess boxing]], a format alternating between speed chess and physical combat.

This tradition serves as both a conflict resolution method and a political tool for gaining influence among the chambers.
