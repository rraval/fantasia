A Planar Rip is a weakening between planes.

## Description

- Weakening of boundary between planes that is slow to heal.
- Objects briefly speed up when passing through.
- Allows passage between planes.
- Visible as distortion.

## Known Locations

### City of Hearts - Occultist Shop

- Party returned to occultist shop in [[City of Hearts]]
- Found shop deserted and wiped clean
- Discovered weakening in back room
- Objects speed up passing through the rip
- Scuff marks and dust patterns examined by [[Hanzo]]
- Things moved IN from the weakening (from other plane)
- Things taken OUT through shop entrance
- [[Prince of Darkness]] may have been connected
- Al the barkeeper sent party there knowing "people don't come back"

### Town of Diamonds - Material Ghetto

- Party used [[Knife of Transposition]] to return ~1500 creatures of [[Lyric]] back home.

## Connections

- [[Knife of Transposition]] leaves behind planar rips when used.
