The multiverse of Fantasia consists of multiple interconnected planes of existence, each with unique properties. Seven planes were involved in the binding of [[Belle]], though not all have been fully explored.

## The Seven Planes

The binding involved seven planes total:

1. Unknown
2. [[Shadowfell]]
3. [[Material Plane]]
4. [[Feywild]]
5. Unknown
6. Unknown
7. Unknown

The [[Elemental Plane]] and [[Orchestral Plane]] are confirmed to exist, but it is unclear where they stand in the hierarchy.

Each plane received a [[Chords of Power|Chord of Power]] after [[Belle]]'s defeat.

## Known Planes

### [[Material Plane]]

The Prime Material Plane is the "normal" reality, home to humans, dwarves, orcs, and other common races. Contains major locations like [[Lyric]], [[Paradise City]], [[Ode]], and [[The Grove]].

- **Time Flow:** Baseline (1x normal)
- **Status:** Subject to [[Material Plane Riots]], partially swapped with [[Feywild]]

### [[Feywild]]

The Realm of Faerie is a parallel plane of wild magic and fae creatures, home to [[Queen of Hearts]].

- **Time Flow:** 2x faster than [[Material Plane]]
- **Politics:** Four houses based on card suits (Hearts, Spades, Diamonds, Clubs)
- **Status:** Partially swapped with [[Material Plane]]

### [[Orchestral Plane]]

Also known as the Dream Plane or Plane of Law, appears as a giant orchestra hall representing cosmic structure.

- **Access:** Through visions and dreams
- **Structure:** Organized like an orchestra with sections for strings, percussion, winds, and conductor
- **Significance:** Shows the three [[Forces]] in their places, represents cosmic order

### [[Shadowfell]]

The Plane of Shadow is a dark reflection of the [[Material Plane]].

- **Geography:** Mirrors [[Material Plane]] locations
- **Exploration:** Briefly encountered, not fully explored

### [[Elemental Plane]]

Realm of Pure Elements, thought to be above [[Feywild]] in cosmic hierarchy.

- **Inhabitants:** Presumably elementals
- **Exploration:** Not directly explored

## Planar Connections

### Transposition

A form of interplanar travel where time passes differently between planes by a factor of 2x. For example, 1 second on the [[Material Plane]] is 2 seconds in the [[Feywild]].

**Methods:**
- [[Knife of Transposition]] - [[Maurice]]'s dagger opens controlled portals
- [[Instrument Panel]] - [[Tinkers Guild]] device that caused the [[Transposition Event]]
- [[Planar Rip]]s - Natural tears allowing passage between planes

### Special Locations

- **[[Tensegrity]]** - Exists in every plane simultaneously

## The Binding

Seven planes were involved in binding [[Belle]] to create the [[Cantum cycle]]. Each plane received a [[Chords of Power|Chord of Power]] after the binding was complete.

The [[Orchestral Plane]] represents the structure of this binding, with different sections corresponding to the three [[Forces]].

## Time Differentials

Different planes experience time at different rates:

- [[Material Plane]]: Baseline (1x)
- [[Feywild]]: 2x faster (2 moons in Feywild = 1 moon on Material)
- Other planes: Unknown

Time differential is visible when viewing one plane from another through portals - voices change pitch and movement appears slowed or accelerated.
