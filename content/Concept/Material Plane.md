The Prime Material Plane is the "normal" reality in Fantasia's multiverse, home to [[Lyric]], [[Paradise City]], and other major locations.

## Characteristics

- Standard time flow (baseline for comparison)
- Home to humans, dwarves, orcs, and other common races
- Contains [[Lyric]], [[Paradise City]], [[Ode]], [[The Grove]]
- Subject to [[Material Plane Riots]]
- Partially swapped with [[Feywild]] in [[Transposition Event]]

## Key Locations

- **[[Lyric]]** - Starting city, site of riots and transposition
- **[[Paradise City]]** - Major city with [[Tensegrity]], showing decay
- **[[Ode]]** - Mining town with [[Cult of Darkness]] mine
- **[[The Grove]]** - Halfling settlement
- Train stations connecting regions

## Time Flow

- Baseline (1x normal)
- Moves half as fast as [[Feywild]]
- When viewed from [[Feywild]] through portal: voices lower pitched, movement appears slowed

## Planar Travel

### [[Transposition Event]]

- [[Tinkers Guild]] [[Instrument Panel|instrument panel]] swapped populations
- ~1500 humans from [[Lyric]] to [[Feywild]]
- Wilderness to Material Plane
- Created [[Planar Rip]] weakening

### [[Knife of Transposition]]

- [[Maurice]]'s dagger opens controlled portals
- Requires soul gems for power
- Can create tears between any planes
- Time differential visible through portals

### Natural Connections

- [[Planar Rip]]s allow passage between planes (see also [[Knife of Transposition]] and [[Instrument Panel]])
- [[Tensegrity]] exists in every plane simultaneously

## Connections

- Part of [[Planar System|seven-plane system]] involved in [[Belle]]'s binding
- Received a [[Chords of Power|Chord of Power]] after [[Belle]]'s defeat
- Connected to [[Feywild]] through [[Transposition Event]]
- Has darker parallel in [[Shadowfell]]
