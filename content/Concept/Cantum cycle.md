> [!INFO] Trivia
>
> "Cantum" comes from "song" in Latin.

The Cantum cycle is a cosmic cycle that [[Maurice]] is bound to conduct, seeking escape from through destroying the instruments.

## Nature

- Cosmic cycle involving music/song
- [[Maurice]] is the conductor bound to it
- Immortality comes with the binding
- Maurice seeks escape from this endless cycle
- Connected to the cosmic ages (Percussion → Strings → Wind)

## Maurice's Role

**The Conductor:**
- Maurice bound the Cantum cycle at some point in history
- Received immortality as consequence
- Must conduct/maintain the cycle
- Seeks release from this burden
- Founded [[The Synth]] but they diverged in vision

**Escape Plan:**
- Wants party to destroy the instruments
- "Kill the instruments, smash them, break them, free me from the cycle"
- If cycle breaks, party can "go back to your own verses"
- Suggests party members not from this verse/reality

## The Cycle

**Evidence of Cycling:**
1. **Percussion Age:** [[Belle]], drums of war
2. **Strings Age:** [[Piano Man]] rules, reason and diplomacy
3. **Wind Age:** Prophesied, "answer blowin' in the wind"
4. Previous mention of "Tambourine man" (percussion champion before Piano Man)

**Pattern:**
- Ages cycle through different cosmic forces
- Each age has champion/god figure
- Transition periods marked by chaos and prophecy
- Instruments tied to each age's power
