The three fundamental Forces govern the D&D universe of Fantasia: Percussion, Strings, and Wind. These cosmic forces cycle through ages, with different champions rising to rule in each era.

## Percussion

**The Drums of War**

Percussion represents war, violence, chaos, and raw physical force. Associated with drums, rhythm, and martial power.

### Characteristics

- **Themes:** War, conquest, violence, chaos, primal energy
- **Instruments:** Drums, percussion instruments
- **Colors/Aesthetics:** Red, blood, raw power
- **Philosophy:** Might makes right, survival of the fittest

### Historical Rule

**Age of the Belle:**
- [[Belle]] (Terribelle) ruled as god of war
- "Ravaged the land" across all 7 planes
- Warfare and conquest dominated
- Contact between planes was common
- Ended when [[Piano Man]] led seven nation army rebellion

**The Binding:**
- [[Belle]]'s power bound using [[Chords of Power]]
- Percussion suppressed for age of Strings
- Influence minimized but not eliminated

### Current Status

- Weakening of Strings creating power vacuum
- [[Drummer Boy]] emerged as undead percussion entity
- Red caps (violent fae) gained political power
- Drums mentioned in [[Material Plane Riots|riots]]: "You cannot hear the drums in your head?"
- [[Belle]]'s bound power possibly resurging
- In Orchestral Plane dream: drums section showed "ripped portals (2,3,4,5)"

### Representatives

- [[Belle]]/Terribelle - ancient god of war
- [[Drummer Boy]] - undead child with drum powers
- Red caps - violent fae who rose during riots
- "Tambourine man" - previous percussion champion before Piano Man

## Strings

**The Order and Diplomacy**

Strings represents reason, order, diplomacy, and structured civilization. Associated with string instruments, harmony, and lawful governance.

### Characteristics

- **Themes:** Order, reason, diplomacy, cooperation, structure
- **Instruments:** Piano, violin, double bass, lyre, string instruments
- **Colors/Aesthetics:** Refined, structured, suspended (tensegrity)
- **Philosophy:** Reason over violence, diplomacy and cooperation

### Historical Rise

**Overthrowing Belle:**
- [[Piano Man|Man of Strings]] united seven nation army
- Organized resistance across all planes
- Eradicated [[Belle]]'s influence through [[The Binding]]
- Established age of reason and diplomacy

**Age of Strings:**
- Hundreds of years of peace
- Planes separated, no contact
- [[Tensegrity]] established with presence in every plane
- Diplomacy replaced warfare
- [[Chords of Power]] maintained order

### Current Status

- [[Piano Man]]'s reign crumbling and ending
- Physically frail, requires [[Tonic]] to maintain power
- [[Prophecy|Augur of Melodies]] proclaimed wind champion rising
- [[Paradise City]] showing decay and deterioration
- [[Uptown Girl]], [[Amras]], and [[Elyas]] attempted [[The Coup]]
- Piano Man foiled the coup by stabbing himself with [[Knife of Transposition]]
- Intentionally trapped his own soul to deny conspirators their prize
- Self-sacrifice act of defiance, quoted [[Fuck You]] as final statement
- In Orchestral Plane dream: strings section strong (2 strong cords)
- Transitioning to Wind age

### Representatives

- [[Piano Man]]/Man of Strings - frail ruler with giant piano
- [[Elyas]] - elven supporter (now disillusioned)

## Wind

**The Change and Transformation**

Wind represents change, transformation, freedom, and the unknown future. Associated with wind instruments, breath, and prophesied renewal.

### Characteristics

- **Themes:** Change, transformation, freedom, prophecy, renewal
- **Instruments:** Pan flutes, wind instruments, brass
- **Colors/Aesthetics:** Movement, air, transformation
- **Philosophy:** Change is inevitable, transformation brings growth

### Prophecy

**Augur's Proclamation:**
- "The answer, my friend, is blowin' in the wind"
- Wind champion prophesied to rise
- Will succeed Strings age
- [[Piano Man]] mused: "it can't be worse than the tambourine man"

**Current Signs:**
- Revolution brewing in [[Paradise City]]
- Winds section in Orchestral Plane dream: "pristine"

### Potential Representatives

- Unknown wind champion yet to fully emerge

### Future Age

The coming age of Wind remains largely unknown, but prophecy suggests:
- Transformation of current order
- [[Piano Man]]'s reign ending peacefully (voluntarily gave up place)
- Possibly more chaotic than Strings but less violent than Percussion
- May involve party members as agents of change
- Return of planar contact/movement

## The Cycle

The three forces cycle through ages in an endless rotation called the [[Cantum cycle]]:

1. **Percussion Age** → Conquered and ruled by force
2. **Strings Age** → Overthrew Percussion, ruled by reason
3. **Wind Age** → Prophesied to succeed Strings, nature unknown
4. **Return to Percussion?** → Cycle continues?

[[Maurice]] is bound as conductor of this cycle, seeking escape by having party destroy the instruments that perpetuate it.
