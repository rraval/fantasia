The Realm of Pure Elements is mentioned as a plane in the cosmic hierarchy above [[Feywild]].

## Known Information

- [[Helia Oriela]] mentioned it's thought to be next plane up from [[Feywild]]
- Presumably home to elementals
- Water elementals on [[Material Plane]] may have connections to this plane
- Not directly explored by party

## Connections

- Part of [[Planar System|seven-plane system]], though exact position in hierarchy unclear
- May have received a [[Chords of Power|Chord of Power]] after [[Belle]]'s defeat
- Connected to elemental manifestations on other planes
