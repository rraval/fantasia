The Plane of Shadow is a dark reflection of the [[Material Plane]], briefly encountered during the campaign.

## Characteristics

- Exists as darker parallel to [[Material Plane]]
- Geography mirrors Material Plane locations
- Has jungle region around [[Lyric]] area

## Chronology

### Combat Encounter

During combat, a bone devil fled to Shadowfell through planar travel, briefly revealing the plane's existence to the party.

### Party's Arrival

- Party emerged from [[Knife of Transposition]] elevator system
- Final elevator embedded into ground at arrival point
- Arrived in mountainous region with distinctive characteristics
- Exited the knife into this plane for the first time

### The Twilight Mountains

**Environment:**
- Mountainous terrain with perpetual twilight
- Sky and ambient light frozen in twilight state
- Neither day nor night, but eternal dusk

**Landmarks:**
- Distant spire smelling of brimstone
- Massive clocktower behind arrival point (ignored by party)
- Party chose to trek toward the spire

**The Whistling Wind:**
- As party traveled, wind picked up around them
- Wind whistles in Shadowfell
- Specifically whistles tune of [[There It Go]] (the Whistle Song)

## Connections

- Part of [[Planar System|seven-plane system]] involved in [[Belle]]'s binding
- Received a [[Chords of Power|Chord of Power]] after [[Belle]]'s defeat
- Listed as second plane in the hierarchy (one below [[Material Plane]])
- Mirrors geography of [[Material Plane]]
