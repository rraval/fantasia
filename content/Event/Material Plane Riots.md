The Material Plane Riots were massive civil unrest in [[Lyric]] that occurred the night before the [[Transposition Event]], marking the beginning of the campaign.

## Timeline

- City of [[Lyric]] on fire
- North section had most fires
- East and west sections had fewer fires
- South section eerily quiet
- Martial law declared, city gates seized by military

## Events

### The Trial

- Party members arrested and tried in courthouse
- [[Vadu]] (Head of Law) presided
- [[Ura]] (Arm of Law) carried out sentences
- [[Darrow]] beheaded for "shooting the sheriff"
- [[The Leader]] and minions interrupted trial
- Leader freed party members
- One minion (baker's apprentice) later burnt down courthouse

### The Escape

- Party fled with orphan children (including [[Drummer Boy]])
- Orphanage was burning
- [[Hanzo]]'s wagon used for transport
- Lord's manor showed single humanoid silhouette
- Land near manor particularly close to Fey
- Party escaped south through gates under martial law

## Connection to Transposition

### Timing

- [[Material Plane Riots]] occurred first
- Party escaped Lyric during the riots
- [[Transposition Event]] happened the morning after party escaped
- [[Feywild Riots]] had occurred 2 full moons earlier (Feywild time)
- Time differential: Feywild moves 2x faster

### Cause and Effect

- [[The Synth]] triggered [[Transposition Event]] when riot mobs threatened their research
- [[Tinkers Guild]] [[Instrument Panel]] was the mechanism used
- Party and commoners witnessed the transposition from outside the city
- Commoners quoted "For What It's Worth" right before it happened
- ~1500 humans transported to Feywild
- Wilderness replaced parts of Lyric

### Aftermath

- [[Vadu]] killed at [[Queen of Hearts]]' ball in Feywild
- [[Ura]] tried to lead Lyric after Vadu's death
- City overrun by jungle/wilderness
- Power structure collapsed
- Humans eventually returned via [[Knife of Transposition]]

## Connections

- [[Transposition Event]] - simultaneous with riots
- [[Feywild Riots]] - occurred earlier in Feywild timeline
- [[Tinkers Guild]] experiment - possible trigger
- Drums in heads mentioned by [[Maurice]] - "You cannot hear the drums in your head? Why do you think the city is on fire!"
