The Ball Massacre was a violent attack at [[Queen of Hearts]]' peace ball where red caps slaughtered attendees including [[8 of Spades]] and [[Vadu]], shattering the Queen's attempt to establish "live and let live" during the [[Feywild Riots]].

## Overview

The Queen of Hearts hosted a grand ball at [[City of Hearts]] with "LIVE AND LET LIVE" heraldry displayed throughout the venue. The ball was meant to establish peace and reconciliation during the tumultuous period of the Feywild Riots. Instead, it became a massacre when red caps attacked and killed multiple attendees.

## Timeline

### Before the Ball

- [[Archer]] arranged for the party to attend with forged invitations
- Guards were told to let the party pass through
- [[Queen of Hearts]] appeared to be under the influence of [[Prince of Darkness]]
- Queen had changed to red livery

### The Event

**Opening:**
- Queen of Hearts entered the ball with [[Prince of Darkness]] accompanying her
- Grand ball with "LIVE AND LET LIVE" heraldry displayed
- [[Vadu]] (Head of Law from the Material Plane) was brought to make a speech about "live and let live"

**The Attack:**
- [[8 of Spades]] raised a toast to begin the festivities
- As he started to speak, a red cap decapitated him mid-toast
- The red cap demanded "Blood"
- Combat erupted throughout the ballroom
- Red caps massacred attendees
- [[Vadu]] was killed by red caps (the theme became "LIVE AND LET DIE")
- [[Prince of Darkness]] was knocked unconscious, then killed
- [[Queen of Hearts]] fell unconscious during the combat

**The Party's Response:**
- Party fought the red caps
- Red caps were killed
- One dying red cap said: "I finally have all the blood I can drink, but it'll never be enough for him"
- Party harvested red cap souls to power the [[Knife of Transposition]]

### Strange Phenomena

When the [[Prince of Darkness]] was killed:
- First reflection shattered
- Second reflection zipped around the priest's body then shattered
- Third reflection zipped around the priest and went into the body
- The body was later taken for examination

## Aftermath

- Queen of Hearts woke from unconsciousness
- Met with the party and [[Archer]] to discuss the crisis
- The massacre demonstrated red caps' unwillingness to accept peace
- Violence showed the extent of political upheaval during [[Feywild Riots]]
- Queen provided resources to help transport humans back to Material Plane

## Significance

- Shattered Queen's attempt at peace and reconciliation
- Demonstrated red caps' violent dominance during the riots
- Killed key political figures including 8 of Spades and Vadu
- Prince of Darkness's influence over Queen ended with his death
- The cryptic dying words suggested red caps served someone with insatiable bloodlust

## Connections

- Part of broader [[Feywild Riots]] chaos
- Occurred at [[City of Hearts]]
- Led to Queen's cooperation with party
- Connected to red caps' political rise
- [[Vadu]]'s death here meant [[Ura]] lost her partner
- Party used red cap souls for transposition magic
