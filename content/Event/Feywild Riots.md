The Feywild Riots occurred two full moons before the [[Material Plane Riots]], resulting in red caps becoming a dominant political force in the Feywild.

## Timeline

Two full moons before Material riots:
- Riots erupted in Feywild
- Red caps organized effectively
- Red caps seized political control
- Power structures destabilized

Same time as Material riots:
- Ace of Spades disappeared with [[Chords of Power|chord of power]]
- [[Lyric]] [[Transposition Event]] occurred
- Humans began appearing in Feywild

## Events and Impact

### Power Shift

- Red caps became dominant political force
- Previously minor/violent faction gained control
- Organized enough to challenge established nobility
- Continued violence at [[Queen of Hearts]]' ball

## Connections to Material Plane

- [[Material Plane Riots]] occurred 2 moons later
- [[Transposition Event]] swapped ~1500 humans from [[Lyric]] to Feywild
- Chaos in both planes may be coordinated or linked
- Red caps' rise coincided with cosmic changes

## Aftermath

### The Ball

- [[Queen of Hearts]]' ball attempted to establish "live and let live"
- Red caps massacred attendees including [[8 of Spades]] and [[Vadu]]
- Violence showed red caps unwilling to accept peace

### Return

- Red caps killed and souls harvested to power [[Knife of Transposition]]
- ~1500 humans returned to Material Plane
- Feywild began recovering from crisis
