The Transposition Event was the [[Tinkers Guild]]'s experiment with the [[Instrument Panel]] that swapped ~1500 humans from [[Lyric]] with wilderness, creating the central crisis of the early campaign.

## The Event

**Location:** [[Lyric]] / [[Town of Diamonds]]

**What Happened:**
- [[Tinkers Guild]] activated [[Instrument Panel]]
- [[Planar rip]] created between Material Plane and Feywild
- ~1500 humans from [[Lyric]] transported to Feywild
- Wilderness/jungle transported to replace parts of Lyric
- Transposition was literal - chunks of Lyric appeared in patches in [[Town of Diamonds]]

## Mechanics

**The Instrument:**
- [[Instrument Panel]] with lyre, drums, pan flutes
- Combined with gems, lenses, mechanical contraptions
- Musical resonance created planar tear
- Similar principle to [[Knife of Transposition]]

**Time Differential Discovered:**
- Feywild time moves 2x faster than Material Plane
- Objects briefly speed up passing through planar weakening
- Voices lower pitched, movement slowed when viewing across planes

**Planar Rip:**
- Weakening of planes at [[Tinkers Guild]] location
- Objects speed up passing through
- Scuff marks showed movement both directions
- Occultist shop had access to it

## Timeline and Effects

**Before:**
- [[Feywild Riots]] occurred 2 full moons earlier
- Red caps rose to power
- Unrest building in Feywild
- [[Material Plane Riots]] broke out in [[Lyric]]
- Party rescued [[Drummer Boy]] and orphans from burning orphanage
- Party escaped Lyric during riots

**At Transposition (morning after riots):**
- [[The Synth]] triggered transposition when riot mobs threatened their research
- Ace of Spades disappeared with [[Chords of Power|chord]]
- Commoners quoted "For What It's Worth"
- Party and commoners witnessed from outside city: earth moved, buildings collapsed, vines shot out
- ~1500 humans appeared in Feywild
- Parts of Lyric appeared in Town of Diamonds
- Jungle/wilderness replaced sections of Lyric

**Aftermath:**
- Humans corralled by military in Town of Diamonds
- Forced into labor
- [[Queen of Hearts]] as acting emissary
- Lyric overrun by jungle
- Party worked to return humans

## Resolution

- Party used [[Knife of Transposition]] to return humans
- Created multiple portals using soul gems
- Four portal tears transported all ~1500 humans
- Easier to maintain reused portals than create new ones
- People warned to leave valuables behind
- All humans successfully returned

## Connections

- [[Instrument Panel]] (caused transposition)
- [[Knife of Transposition]] (solved it)
- [[Pan Flute]] from [[Viscount Bridgerton]] (one of the instruments)
