The Coup was an attempt by [[Uptown Girl]], [[Amras]], and [[Elyas]]' elven forces to overthrow the [[Piano Man]] after [[The Intervals]], which resulted in Piano Man's capture through the [[Knife of Transposition]].

## Overview

After the party became champions of [[The Intervals]], they found themselves entangled in a coup conspiracy against the [[Piano Man]]. The coup was led by [[Uptown Girl]] (Piano Man's romantic partner) and [[Amras]] (his healer), secretly funded by [[Elyas]]—the same elven leader who originally helped Piano Man overthrow [[Belle]] but had become disillusioned with his crumbling reign.

## Background

### The Conspirators

**[[Elyas]]:**
- Originally funded Piano Man's rebellion against [[Belle]]
- Became disillusioned as Piano Man's reign deteriorated and [[Paradise City]] fell into decay
- Secretly funded the coup through Uptown Girl and Amras
- Brother of [[Vadu]] (Head of Law)

**[[Uptown Girl]]:**
- Piano Man's romantic partner
- Had an affair with [[Amras]]
- Operated from within [[Tensegrity]], using her position of authority
- Leader of the coup forces

**[[Amras]]:**
- Piano Man's healer and cleric
- Working with Uptown Girl
- Had access to Piano Man through his role providing [[Tonic]]

## Timeline

### The Setup

- Elyas funded the conspiracy from his elven kingdom
- Uptown Girl and Amras positioned themselves close to Piano Man
- Party became champions of [[The Intervals]], gaining Piano Man's trust and banner

### The Coup Attempt

- Party found itself entangled in the coup after winning [[The Intervals]]
- Coup forces moved against Piano Man at [[Tensegrity]]
- Rather than let the conspirators capture and control him, Piano Man chose defiance

**Piano Man's Defiant Act:**
- Stabbed himself with the [[Knife of Transposition]]
- Intentionally trapped his own soul in the knife's gem
- Left behind only a physical shell
- Denied [[Uptown Girl]] and [[Amras]] their prize by removing himself from the board
- See [[Fuck You]] for his final statement

**Party's Diversion:**
- Used the [[Dress of Love]] to distract and divert the usurpers
- Bought themselves a few hours to rest
- The magical disguise allowed them to move freely

### Aftermath

**Immediate Consequences:**
- Uptown Girl and Amras retreated to Uptown district of Tensegrity
- They denied Elyas' involvement: "the wildest things that there is no Elyas here"
- Guards were confused about the conspiracy's origins
- Piano Man's physical presence gone, but soul trapped in knife

**Party's Dream:**
- Party entered [[Planes#Orchestral|Orchestral Plane]] (dream plane)
- Appeared as giant orchestra hall
- Met fragmented version of Piano Man in strings section
- Piano Man had incoherent ramblings and no memory of recent events

**The Escape:**
- Party woke to find their previous tricks no longer worked on guards
- Escaped [[Tensegrity]] as [[Paradise City]] fell into chaos
- [[Maurice]] picked them up in his caravan
- [[Water Elemental Revolution]] was brewing in the disintegrating city

**Tensegrity's Fall:**
- After the party escaped, [[Tensegrity]] collapsed into a heap
- The floating fortress fell and its wards were broken
- Desertification began spreading through the area
- [[Uptown Girl]] took control of the ruins

## Significance

- Ended Piano Man's direct reign over the planes
- Exposed the fragility of the age of Strings and diplomacy
- [[Elyas]] turned against the system he helped create
- Piano Man's capture created power vacuum that [[Forces#Percussion|percussion forces]] exploited
- Party gained possession of Piano Man's soul in the [[Knife of Transposition]]

## Connections

- Followed directly after [[The Intervals]]
- Connected to [[Piano Man]]'s declining power
- Part of broader collapse of Strings' dominance
- Led to [[Water Elemental Revolution]] gaining momentum
- Created opening for [[Forces#Percussion|drums of war]] to return
- [[Vadu]]'s death occurred during this tumultuous period
