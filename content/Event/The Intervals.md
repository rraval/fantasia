The Intervals is a competition/tournament held by [[Piano Man|Man of Strings]] where the party competed and became champions, facing elimination challenges while being targeted by [[The Synth]].

## Overview

- Tournament organized by [[Piano Man]] to determine champions
- Competitors need banner/invitation to participate
- Party competed under Piano Man's banner as "Saviors of [[Lyric]], Slayers of the Deep"
- Elimination-style competition with multiple challenges
- Teams cannot damage each other but can interfere in non-damaging ways
- [[The Synth]] targeted competitors throughout the event

## The Teams

Four teams competed in The Intervals:

1. **The Party** - "Saviors of Lyric, Slayers of the Deep"
   - Competed under [[Piano Man]]'s banner

2. **[[The Champions]]** - Led by [[Freddy Mercury]]
   - [[Freddy Mercury]] (actually a Couatl in disguise using Change Shape)
   - Human followers

3. **[[The Children of the World]]** - Elf warriors and mages
   - Associated with [[Elyas]]
   - Eliminated in first challenge

4. **[[My Anaconda Don't]]** - Yuan-Ti warriors and mages
   - Later killed by [[The Synth]] between challenges

## Timeline

### Invitation

- Party brought [[Augur of Melodies]] to [[Piano Man]]
- Piano Man, empowered by a [[Tonic]] from [[Amras]], performed a ritual with the Augur
- The Augur delivered a prophecy about the winds before dying
- Piano Man granted party the honor of competing under his banner
- This was in exchange for saving the Augur
- Party stayed at an inn where the banner granted them luxurious accommodations

### Challenge 1: The Tea Relay

- [[Grondar Blackfloyd]] brought the party to the opening ceremony
- Large circular stadium with a track around a central field
- Three pillars placed around the track with different terrain sections:
  - Regular road
  - Sand
  - Water
- Each team nominated a champion to race

**The Challenge:**
- Champions must collect items from each pillar and combine them
- First pillar: tea bag
- Second pillar: sugar
- Third pillar: water
- Make tea and present it to Piano Man
- Last team to complete gets eliminated

**The Champions:**
- Party: [[Helia Oriela]] (wild shaped into an Ape)
- The Champions: [[Freddy Mercury]] (revealed Couatl form immediately)
- The Children of the World: Elven mage (used dimension door)
- My Anaconda Don't: Yuan-Ti warrior

**The Race:**
- Freddy Mercury calmly shapeshifted into a Couatl and flew to the first pillar
- Helia successfully rode Freddy after they both reached the first pillar
- Freddy tried to throw her off with a loop-de-loop but failed
- The elf ran the course backwards, strong start but slowed down
- The Yuan-Ti struggled to climb, using fangs as holds
- Helia rode Freddy to collect all items and used flame spells to quickly make tea

**Results:**
1. Party (Helia) - first place
2. The Champions (Freddy) - second place, shapeshifted back to human form
3. My Anaconda Don't - third place (party negotiated with them to sabotage the elves)
4. The Children of the World - **eliminated**

[[Piano Man]] tasted the tea and recited "Pennyroyal Tea":
> Sit and drink Pennyroyal Tea
> Distill the life that's inside of me
> Sit and drink Pennyroyal Tea
> I'm anemic royalty

[[Uptown Girl]] appeared upset by the elves' elimination.

### Interlude: The Valentine Drama

- Party returned to the inn after winning the first challenge
- The inn performed a drama celebrating the new winners
- Drama told the tragic love story of [[Rose]], [[Jack]], [[DeWitt]], and [[Princess of Diamonds|Bonnie]]
- Explained the origin of the [[Dress of Love]]

### Synth Attack at the Inn

- Water elementals gathered at the inn to behold [[Cory|Corminar]]
- They called him "Tide Turner" and "Champion of the Undertow"
- [[Lucius]] joined the party and bought drinks
- [[The Synth]] sent assassins to attack the inn: **The Theremin Twins**
  - A Yochlol strumming a theremin
  - A werebear (transformed from mist)
- They specifically targeted [[Helia Oriela]]
- Party defeated them quickly with moonbeam, effective against shapeshifters

**Revelations:**
- The Synth is led by the "Fifth Prophet"
- The Synth was attacking all competing teams
- [[My Anaconda Don't]] (Yuan-Ti team) was killed off and ravaged

**Loot:**
- Perfume of Bewitching
- Wand of Scowling (from a severed hand)
- Ring of Free Action (from the Yochlol)

### Challenge 2: Capture the Flag

- A capture the flag challenge with [[Freddy Mercury]] and an angelic companion
- Complex location with time signature based shifting elevators and pits

## Aftermath

- Party became champions of The Intervals
- Earned title/recognition as "The Champions" or similar
- Banner of [[Piano Man|Man of Strings]] granted them authority and access throughout his domain
- Demonstrated their capabilities to the major powers of Strings

## Connections

- [[Piano Man]] organized the event
- [[The Synth]] and the Fifth Prophet targeted competitors
- [[Freddy Mercury]] revealed himself as a Couatl
- [[Uptown Girl]] observed the competition
- [[Grondar Blackfloyd]] served as liaison
- [[Amras]] provided a [[Tonic]] that empowered Piano Man
