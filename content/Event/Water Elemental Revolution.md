The Water Elemental Revolution is an uprising in [[Paradise City]] instigated by [[Cory|Corminar]], who was recognized by water elementals as the "Tide Turner, Champion of the Undertow."

## Background

Water elementals power steam engines by being continuously boiled and condensed, providing the [[Piano Man]] with cheap, effective industrialization.

## Key Events

**Discovery:**: Train "City Boy" entering [[Paradise City]]
- The party fought with assassins from [[The Synth]] attacking the [[Augur of Melodies]]
- The fight ended in the cab, where a water elemental was seen powering the engine
- As the party negotiated with [[Grondar]], Cory suggested to the elemental that they were being enslaved by the Man of Strings and should fight for their rights

**Recognition:** Inn in [[Paradise City]]

- Water elementals gathered at inn to behold [[Cory|Corminar]]
- Recognized him as "Tide Turner"
- Acclaimed him as "Champion of the Undertow"
- [[Lucius]] joined party at this gathering
- [[The Synth]] assassins ambushed the inn during this moment:
  - Mist creature attacked [[Helia Oriela]], turned into werebear
  - Yochlol strumming theremin also attacked
  - Party fought them off

**Revolution:** [[Paradise City]], as the party escapes a crumbling [[Tensegrity]]

- Train revolution actively happening
- Water elementals withdrew from powering trains
- Party escaped [[Tensegrity]] amid the chaos
- Used pass without trace to navigate the revolutionary chaos
