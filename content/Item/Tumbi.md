The Tumbi is a traditional Punjabi stringed instrument played by half of the [[Piano Man]] inside the [[Knife of Transposition]].

## Description

- Traditional stringed instrument from Punjab
- Single string plucked to create distinctive high-pitched sound
- Featured prominently in Punjabi folk and bhangra music
- This particular Tumbi was the only instrument available to half of the [[Piano Man]] in his confinement
- Destroyed beyond mundane repair

## Mechanics

**Sound:**
- Single metal string creates distinctive resonant tone
- Produces rhythmic, percussive melodic patterns
- Often used to accompany vocals in traditional music

**Magical Properties:**
- This Tumbi possessed unusually strong magic
- Mundane *mend* spell insufficient to restore destroyed string
- Magic likely tied to [[Piano Man]]'s essence or the [[Knife of Transposition]]

**Aftermath:**

- Attempted to convince party to restore him to power
- Dismissed party when they refused, pointing to cylindrical elevator
