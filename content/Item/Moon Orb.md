The Moon Orb is a magical sphere that acts as both reservoir and lens for moonlight.

## Description

- Orb that pulses softly with light
- Acts as reservoir storing moonlight
- Functions as lens focusing moonlight
- Required to materialize [[Dress of Love]]

## Mechanics

**Function:**
- Stores moonlight energy in reservoir
- Focuses/lenses moonlight for magical effects
- Pulses with stored energy
- Can be refilled with moonbeam spell

**Dress Activation:**
- Light streams from orb when near dress
- Coalesces scattered light into physical garment
- Must be present for dress to materialize

## Chronology

**Discovery:**
- [[Cory]] searched [[Princess of Diamonds]]' mansion office
- Found desk drawer with false bottom
- Discovered Moon Orb pulsing softly with light
- Brought orb to closet
- Light streamed out and materialized [[Dress of Love]]
- Party took both orb and dress

**Analysis:**
- [[Louis Orrai]] performed arcana checks
- Determined orb acts as both reservoir and lens for moonlight
- Compared to moonstone earring (lens only, no reservoir)
- Explained how dress works with orb's power

**Refill Attempt:**
- [[Helia Oriela]] cast moonbeam spell
- Successfully refilled the Moon Orb
- Refill had no effect on [[Dress of Love]] appearance
- Suggests dress needs specific lunar moonlight, not spell-generated

## Connections

- Powers [[Dress of Love]]
- Moonstone earring similar but weaker (lens only)
- Fae magic tied to lunar cycles
- Green Hag ([[Princess of Diamonds]]) had possessed it
