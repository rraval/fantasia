A tonic is a luminescent potion, allowing consumers to tap into the rhythms of the universe.

## Known Tonics

### Blue

[[Amras]] presents a blue tonic to the [[Piano Man]]:

- Undergoes physical transformation
- Can tap into rhythms of the universe
- Eyes blaze with life
- Able to play giant piano
- Can force prophecies through musical power, duet with [[Augur of Melodies]]
- Augur proclaimed "answer is blowin' in the wind" and died
- After effects wear off, reverts to sickly thin state
- Physically exerted after use
