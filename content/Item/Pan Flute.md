The Pan Flute is a musical instrument found in [[Viscount Bridgerton]]'s chest, rigged with gem stones, lenses, and mechanical contraptions similar to the [[Tinkers Guild]]'s [[Instrument Panel]].

## Description

- Pan flute (wind instrument)
- Rigged with gem stones
- Contains lenses
- Has mechanical contraptions
- Not intrinsically magical, but resonates with magic
- Holes appear blocked

## Mechanics

- Blowing into flute does nothing (holes blocked)
- Not intrinsically magical
- Resonates with magic when examined
- Similar principle to [[instrument panel]] and [[Knife of Transposition]]

## Chronology

**Discovery:**
- Party stole [[Item/Ciphered Note in Ode]] from [[Viscount Bridgerton]]'s saddle bags
- Searched Viscount's rooms
- Found chest containing Pan Flute
- Examined and found it rigged with gems, lenses, mechanical parts
- Blowing into flute had no effect
- Holes appeared to be blocked somehow

## Connections

- Missing piece of the [[Instrument Panel]]
- [[Knife of Transposition]] - uses gems and souls for portals
- [[Viscount Bridgerton]] connected to transposition experiments
