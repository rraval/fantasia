The Chords of Power are powerful artifacts used by the [[Piano Man]] to defeat [[Belle]], with one chord given to each of the seven nations/planes.

## Description

- Powerful artifacts
- Seven chords total (one per plane/nation)
- Hold/bind cosmic forces
- Connected to musical/harmonic magic

## Distribution

After [[Belle]]'s defeat:
- Seven nation army each received a Chord of Power
- House of Spades held chord for Feywild realm
- Each plane/nation had their own chord
- Used to bind Belle's power and maintain separation

### Connections

- [[Feywild Riots]] result in power shift
- Ace of Spades disappeared with Feywild's Chord of Power
- Same time as [[Lyric]] transposition
- Connection between chord's disappearance and transposition unclear
