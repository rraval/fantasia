The Dress of Love is a magical garment that makes viewers see the wearer as the person they love most, powered by moonlight.

## Description

- Shiny dress that materializes in moonlight
- Makes creatures see wearer as person they love most
- Powered by moonlight
- Effect diminishes on second use in same location or with same person (unclear)

## Origin

The Dress of Love was created from a tragic love story involving [[Princess of Diamonds|Bonnie]], [[Rose]], [[Jack]], and [[DeWitt]]:

**The Love Triangle:**
- [[Princess of Diamonds|Bonnie]] was a seamstress who loved [[Jack]]
- Jack fell in love with [[Rose]], daughter of seamstress [[DeWitt]]
- Consumed by jealousy, Bonnie orchestrated a deadly deception

**The Deception:**
- Bonnie lied to DeWitt, telling him Jack was a vampire
- Bonnie lied to Jack, telling him Rose would meet him at [[Wise Men Falls]]
- DeWitt killed Jack with a crossbow bolt through the heart
- Rose died of grief, becoming a ghost

**The Creation:**
- Rose's spirit haunted the [[Love Shack]], waiting for Jack
- Jack's spirit remained trapped at [[Wise Men Falls]], unable to move on
- When Rose learned the truth about Jack's death, she finally found peace
- Rose possessed the water wheel at the seamstress cottage
- Her heart strings fed the loom
- A single bolt of moonlight cloth was produced: the Dress of Love
- Bonnie (the [[Princess of Diamonds|Green Hag]]) took the magical cloth

This origin story was depicted in a drama performed at an inn during [[The Intervals]]. The dress embodies Rose's eternal love and sacrifice.

## Mechanics

**Activation:**
- Requires a source of moonlight (the moon itself, or any reservoir)
- Dress appears as twinkles of light without orb
- Light streams from the source and coalesces into dress
- Once materialized, can be worn

**Effect:**
- Viewers see wearer as person they love most
- Works on different creatures differently
- Scout saw [[Helia Oriela]] wearing it as [[Queen of Hearts]]
- Does not work as well the second time (possibly same place or person)

**Power Source:**
- [[Moon Orb]] acts as reservoir and lens for moonlight
- Moonstone earring also works as lens (lesser effect)
- Moonbeam spell successfully refilled Moon Orb
- Refill had no effect on dress appearance

## Chronology

**Discovery:**
- Party infiltrated [[Princess of Diamonds]]' mansion to steal it
- Found closet with twinkles of light but no dress
- [[Cory]] found [[Moon Orb]] in desk with false bottom
- Brought orb to closet
- Light streamed from orb, coalescing into shiny dress
- [[Helia Oriela]] put on dress "gleaming with rabbit joy"
- During escape, scout saw Helia as [[Queen of Hearts]]
- Confused scout: "Why is Queen of Hearts attacking him?"
- Party escaped using pass without trace

**Analysis:**
- [[Louis Orrai]] performed arcana checks
- Determined orb acts as reservoir and lens for moonlight
- Moonstone earring acts only as lens
- Dress makes creatures see wearer as person they love most
- [[Helia Oriela]] cast moonbeam, refilled [[Moon Orb]]
- Refill had no effect on dress

**Diminished Effect:**
- Post coup, Helia reused the dress
- Dress did not work as well the second time
- Unclear if problem was location or person-specific
