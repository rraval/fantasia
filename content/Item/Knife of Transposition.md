The Knife of Transposition is [[Maurice]]'s dagger that can open portals between planes using gems infused with souls.

## Description

- Dagger owned by [[Maurice]]
- Can carve tears in reality to create [[Planar Rip]]s
- Requires gems infused with souls to power
- Gems fuse with hilt when activated
- Energy expenditure based on portal size and duration

## Mechanics

**Portal Creation:**
- Wielder holds gem and carves tear in reality
- Gem magically fuses with knife's hilt
- Energy feeds from gem into portal
- Moving knife to expand tear expends more energy
- Inch-long gem expended in ~2 minutes of maintaining portal
- Reusing same tear is easier to maintain than creating new one

**Powering:**
- Requires souls trapped in gems
- Souls obtained by killing reflections after separating them from host
- Each gem provides limited energy based on size
- Multiple gems needed for large-scale transport

**Time Differential:**
- Feywild time moves 2x faster than Material Plane
- Portal shows this effect - voices pitch-shifted, movement slowed

## Chronology

**First Mention:**
- [[Maurice]] joined party
- Carried the knife (not yet demonstrated)

**First Demonstration**
- Party demonstrated knife to [[Queen of Hearts]]
- [[Hanzo]] wielded Maurice's knife
- Held gem and carved tear into reality
- Gem fused with hilt, feeding energy into portal
- Hanzo pulled downwards to expand tear
- Accidentally sliced through person on other side
- Revealed [[Ev]] on Material Plane side
- Time differential observed: Ev moved 2x slower, voice lower pitched
- [[Louis Orrai]] measured energy expenditure
- Inch-long gem expended in about 2 minutes
- Portal closed when gem depleted

**Soul Harvesting Process:**
- Tie down humanoid
- Cast suggestion on their reflection
- Separate reflection from host
- Kill reflection to trap soul in gem
- Party used red caps provided by Queen

**Hanzon:**
- [[Hanzo]] returned to Feywild via [[Maurice]]: "If I give you a dagger, I expect you to hold on to it"

**Saviors of [[Lyric]]:**
- Party transported ~1500 humans from [[Town of Diamonds]] back to Material Plane
- First tear: ~200 people (caused trample, took valuables)
- Reused tear easier to maintain: ~400 women with children
- Third reuse: ~600 people
- Fourth reuse: remaining people
- Used multiple soul gems (5 red caps harvested)

**Piano Man's Self-Sacrifice:**
- [[Piano Man]] stabbed himself to foil [[The Coup]]
- Intentionally trapped his own soul to deny the conspirators their prize
- Act of defiance against [[Uptown Girl]], [[Amras]], and [[Elyas]]
- Quoted [[Fuck You]] as his final statement
- Knife now contains at least part of his soul
