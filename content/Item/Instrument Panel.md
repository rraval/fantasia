The instrument panel is a musical contraption built by [[Tinkers Guild]] featuring lyre, drums, and a pan flute that caused the [[Transposition Event]] swapping parts of [[Lyric]] with wilderness.

## Description

- Musical contraption with multiple instruments
- Contains lyre, drums, and pan flutes
- Built by [[Tinkers Guild]] in [[Lyric]]
- Capable of causing planar transposition

## Function

**Transposition Effect:**
- Created planar rifts between Material Plane and Feywild
- Swapped ~1500 humans from [[Lyric]] with wilderness
- Parts of Lyric literally transposed into [[Town of Diamonds]]
- Created weakening of planes ([[Planar Rip]])
- Time differential: Feywild moves 2x faster than Material Plane

## Chronology

### The Experiment

- [[Tinkers Guild]] conducted experiment
- Activated instrument panel
- Successfully created transposition
- ~1500 humans transported to Feywild
- Wilderness replaced urban sections of Lyric
- Created ongoing [[Planar Rip]] effect

### Aftermath

- Lyric overrun by jungle/wilderness
- Humans corralled in [[Town of Diamonds]]
- [[Material Plane Riots]] occurred the night before transposition
- [[The Synth]] triggered transposition when riot mobs threatened their research
- Party witnessed transposition from outside city the morning after escaping
- Party eventually returned humans using [[Knife of Transposition]]

## Connections

- [[Knife of Transposition]] (gems + souls for portals)
- [[Feywild Riots]] occurred 2 full moons before Material riots
- [[Material Plane Riots]] at time of transposition
