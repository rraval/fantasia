Darrow is a human fighter who appeared in both the main campaign and a oneshot. He is infamous for "shooting the sheriff".

## Chronology

### The Sheriff Incident

- At the bar with [[Sir Questor Imperialus]]
- When a masked elf dragged a wrapped body (tied-up tiefling) into a back room
- After [[Dwollin]] stumbled into the room revealing torture in progress, Darrow leapt over the bar for cover
- Readied an arrow to be shot if the elf harmed anyone else
- When the elf knocked the tiefling prisoner unconscious, Darrow fired his prepared arrow
- His arrow killed the elf
- The busboy exclaimed: "you bastards, you shot the sheriff"

### Trial in Lyric

- Arrested and tried alongside the main party members
- Accused of "shooting the sheriff"
- Sentenced to fight a duel with [[Ura]] (the Arm of Law) for his freedom
- Was beheaded by Ura, splattering blood on the "LAW ONE" sign
- His death occurred right before [[The Leader]] and minions interrupted the trial
