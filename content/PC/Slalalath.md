Slalalath is an elf wizard who appeared in a oneshot.

## Background

Slalalath was sitting in a booth at a bar in a small town when a masked elf dragged a wrapped body (a tied-up tiefling) into a back room.

## Chronology

### Shot the Sheriff

- Sitting in a booth at a bar
- Perceived the wrapped body as alive and still moving under its own volition
- When a dwarf ([[Dwollin]]) stumbled into the back room revealing torture, cast ray of frost on the elf (dealing 7 damage)
- Ducked under their table for cover
- Was grappled by [[Sir Questor Imperialus]] but broke free
- Disengaged and ran outside to peer through a window, leaving the door open
- The elf smashed his club on the tiefling prisoner, and [[Darrow]] shot and killed the elf
- The busboy revealed "you bastards, you shot the sheriff"
