Louis Orrai is a wizard searching for the "song of the universe". He joined the party during their travels after escaping [[Lyric]].

## Background

Louis was found failing to hide behind some bushes near [[Ode]]. He introduced himself as looking for the song of the universe and wanted to join the party because "the party's footfalls are missing a beat" and he wanted to fill it in.
