"Welcome to the Jungle" by Guns N' Roses was referenced in [[Lyric]] after the [[Transposition Event]].

## Description

After Lyric was transposed with wilderness, the city became overrun by jungle. During combat with a dryad in this jungle-transformed Lyric, the dryad taunted the party with the song's threatening lyrics: "welcome to the jungle baby, you're gonna die." The line "watch them bleed" from the song was used to describe the dryad's violent intentions.
