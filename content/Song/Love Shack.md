"Love Shack" by The B-52's is the namesake for the [[Love Shack]], the cottage where [[Rose]]'s ghost haunted while waiting for [[Jack]].

## Description

The song's lyrics "The love shack is a little old place where we can get together" described the cottage that became the center of Rose and Jack's tragic love story. The location was literally called the Love Shack, where Rose's spirit remained after her death from grief.
