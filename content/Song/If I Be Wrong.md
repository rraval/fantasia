"If I Be Wrong" by Wolf Larsen was quoted by [[David Blane]] when uncertain about marriage.

## Description

The song's haunting question "What if I'm wrong?" captured David's depression and self-doubt as he ran away from his own wedding for the third time. He worried about "signing bride up for life" with someone as uncertain as himself.
