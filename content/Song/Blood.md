"Blood" was referenced during [[The Ball Massacre]] at the [[Queen of Hearts]]' ball.

## Description

When [[8 of spades]] raised a toast at the Queen's ball, red caps decapitated him while demanding "Blood." The red caps continued their massacre, with one spitting out "I finally have all the blood I can drink, but it'll never be enough for him."

## Connections

- [[Queen of Hearts]] - At her ball where the massacre occurred
- [[The Ball Massacre]] - The violent event
- Red caps - Demanded and spilled blood
- [[Prince of Darkness]] - The "him" who can never get enough blood
