"The Joker" by Steve Miller Band represents [[Maurice]], the immortal conductor who founded [[The Synth]].

## Description

The song's themes of freedom, transformation, and playful rebellion fit Maurice's character perfectly. Maurice quotes the song and is known by its title as one of his aliases.

## Connections

- First meeting at crossroads outside [[Lyric]]
- Referenced when Maurice appeared on train to [[Paradise City]]
