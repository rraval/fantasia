"Paradise City" by Guns N' Roses is the namesake for [[Song/Paradise City Song]], the Piano Man's capital city.

## Description

The song's title contrasts ironically with the actual state of the city - while named after a paradise, Paradise City is described as run down with strings holding it together and people who are not well off.
