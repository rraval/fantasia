"Don't Stop Believin'" by Journey is referenced through the train system serving [[Paradise City]].

## Description

The song's characters "Small Town Girl" and "City Boy" became the names of locomotives. The "Small Town Girl" train was in for repairs, while "City Boy" transported the party to Paradise City.
