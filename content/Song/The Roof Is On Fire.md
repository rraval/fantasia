"The Roof Is on Fire" is a soca song referenced during multiple fire-related encounters.

## Description

The song's urgent refrain appeared both during the orphanage fire rescue in [[Lyric]] (where party saved [[Drummer Boy]] and other children) and when the party triggered a massive cone of flame trap in a dungeon.

## Connections

- [[Lyric]] - Burning orphanage during [[Material Plane Riots]]
- [[Drummer Boy]] - Rescued from the burning orphanage
- [[Follow The Leader]] - Also a soca song, connected thematically
- Flame trap encounter in dungeon
