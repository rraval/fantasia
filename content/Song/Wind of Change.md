"Wind of Change" by the Scorpions is a power ballad about hope and transformation, sung by [[Flo|Master Flo]] to rally his forces during the battle in the [[Shadowfell]].

## Description

During the clocktower battle (see [[34 - Main 30]]), the party heard a chorus carried on [[Flo|Master Flo]]'s voice turning the tide of his fight. The chorus invoked "the wind of change" and, specifically, "children of tomorrow," and as the music's pitch suddenly dropped it conjured a 40-foot bubble of slowed time around Flo's banner, forcing enemies to retreat.

The song reads as a manifestation of the prophesied [[Forces#Wind|Wind age]] of change now displacing the [[Piano Man]]'s waning Strings age.

A History check tied the lyric "children of tomorrow" to the orphanage of orphans in [[Lyric]] - the burning orphanage the party rescued [[Drummer Boy]] and the other children from during the [[Material Plane Riots]] - suggesting a connection between the song and those children.
