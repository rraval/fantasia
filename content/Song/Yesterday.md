"Yesterday" by The Beatles was performed by half of the [[Piano Man]] inside the [[Knife of Transposition]].

## Description

When the party was pulled into the [[Knife of Transposition]], they found themselves in a small translucent cavern with foggy edges. Inside, they encountered a solitary figure - half of the [[Piano Man]] - playing a [[Tumbi]] and singing "Yesterday... all my troubles were so far away." The melancholic performance was cut short when [[Helia]] accidentally destroyed the [[Tumbi]] with a fireball, leaving the Piano Man without his only instrument.

## Connections

- [[Piano Man]] - Performed this song inside the knife as half of himself
- [[Knife of Transposition]] - Location where the song was performed
- [[Tumbi]] - The instrument used to play this song, later destroyed
- [[Helia]] - Destroyed the Tumbi during the performance
