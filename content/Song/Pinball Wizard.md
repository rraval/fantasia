"Pinball Wizard" by The Who is connected to [[The Synth]], the [[Bally Table]], and [[Bob]].

## Description

The song inspired the character Tommy, first leader of [[The Synth]], who established the [[Bally Table]]. The party encountered this massive pinball machine dungeon where [[Stodor]] was chained and forced to throw pinballs, and [[Bob]] - a literal wizard - was trapped inside the bumpers.

## Connections

- Tommy - First leader of [[The Synth]], now deceased
- [[Bally Table]] - Pinball-themed dungeon
- [[The Synth]] - Organization founded around this theme
- [[Bob]] - A wizard literally trapped in the pinball machine
- [[Stodor]] - Chained to operate the machine
- [[Viscount Bridgerton]] - Operated the facility
