"Pennyroyal Tea" by Nirvana was quoted literally during the first event of [[The Intervals]].

## Description

The Tea Relay challenge at The Intervals involved collecting tea bags, sugar, and water to make tea. [[Piano Man]] tasted the winning tea and recited the song's lyrics: "Sit and drink Pennyroyal Tea / Distill the life that's inside of me / Sit and drink Pennyroyal Tea / I'm anemic royalty."
