"Get your freak on" by Missy Elliott features the [[Tumbi]], a traditional Punjabi stringed instrument, in its distinctive production.

## Description

Producer Timbaland incorporated the Tumbi's sound into the song's innovative beat, blending South Asian musical elements with hip-hop. The distinctive sound of this traditional instrument became part of one of Missy Elliott's most recognizable tracks.
