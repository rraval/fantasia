"For What's It's Worth" by Buffalo Springfield was quoted by commoners right before they witnessed the [[Transposition Event]].

## Description

The morning after the party escaped [[Lyric]] during the [[Material Plane Riots]], a group of commoners approached their camp asking what happened in the city. One quoted the song's famous opening "There's something happening here, what it is ain't exactly clear." Immediately after, the earth moved, buildings collapsed, and gigantic vines shot out overrunning the city with wilderness - the [[Transposition Event]] happening before their eyes.
