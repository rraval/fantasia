"Jump" by Van Halen was used as a puzzle during the party's encounter with [[Viscount Bridgerton]] at the [[Bally Table]].

## Description

The party triggered a floor trap inscribed with lyrics "I go up, and nothing ever gets me down." The floor began rising toward ceiling spikes while Bridgerton taunted over the PA. The party had to realize they needed to jump simultaneously to make the floor drop instead of rise, repeating this to reach the second airlock.

## Connections

- [[Viscount Bridgerton]] - Operated the trap and taunted the party
- [[Bally Table]] - Location of the puzzle
