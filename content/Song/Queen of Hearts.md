"Queen of Hearts" by Juice Newton inspired the character [[Queen of Hearts]], a fae ruler in the [[Feywild]].

## Description

The song's themes of love, games, and emotional manipulation align with the Queen of Hearts character. She was consort to the King of Hearts and has been "clutching at lost power" since his death, managing power struggles with other fae houses while maintaining her position as a ruler (not the ruler of the entire Feywild).

## Connections

- [[Queen of Hearts]] - Named after this song, a ruler among many in the Feywild
- [[Feywild]] - Where she rules as one of several fae powers
- King of Hearts - Her deceased consort
- Power struggles with other fae houses
