"Bad Moon Rising" by Creedence Clearwater Revival is central to [[Stodor]]'s tragic backstory.

## Description

The song's lyrics "Don't go around tonight / Well it's bound to take your life / I know the end is comin' soon" described the night an unnatural golden-red moon appeared - actually a [[Synth Satellite]] that raised waves of undead. This led to the death of Stodor's family and his eventual capture.

## Connections

- [[Stodor]] - The Bad Moon Rising is the title of his backstory
- [[The Synth]] - Their satellite caused the undead waves
