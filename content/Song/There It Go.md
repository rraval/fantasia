"There It Go (The Whistle Song)" by Juelz Santana is known for its distinctive whistled melody, which the party heard echoed in the winds of the [[Shadowfell]].

## Description

When the party emerged from the [[Knife of Transposition]] into the [[Shadowfell]], they found themselves in a mountainous region with perpetual twilight. As they trekked toward a distant spire, the wind picked up around them. They discovered that in the Shadowfell, the wind whistles - specifically, it whistles the iconic melody from "There It Go," creating an eerie atmosphere in the shadowy plane.

## Connections

- [[Shadowfell]] - The wind in this plane whistles this song's melody
- [[Knife of Transposition]] - The party arrived in Shadowfell via this item
