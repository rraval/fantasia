"Anaconda" by Nicki Minaj (which samples "Baby Got Back") inspired [[My Anaconda Don't]], a faction of Yuan-Ti warriors and mages.

## Description

The song's memorable hook "My anaconda don't want none" was adapted into the faction name, fitting a Yuan-Ti (snake people) organization.
