"Save The Children" was quoted by [[Sam]] after the party discovered what happened to the orphans at the [[Cult of Darkness]] mines.

## Description

The session ended with a Sam POV quoting this song after the party found the horrific truth: [[Viscount Bridgerton]] had sold the orphan children to the [[Cult of Darkness]], where they were tortured. This led to [[Drummer Boy]]'s transformation and the deaths of the other children.
