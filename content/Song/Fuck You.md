"Fuck You" (also known as "Forget You") by Cee Lo Green was quoted by [[Piano Man|Man of Strings]] when he confronted [[Uptown Girl]] about her affair with [[Amras]].

## Description

The Piano Man quoted this song in a moment of betrayal and defiance, right before stabbing himself with the [[Knife of Transposition]] to foil [[The Coup|Uptown Girl, Amras, and Elyas' coup attempt]]. Rather than let them capture and control him, he intentionally trapped his own soul, denying them their prize.

## Connections

- [[Piano Man]] - Quoted the song during confrontation
- [[Uptown Girl]] - His romantic partner who betrayed him
- [[Amras]] - Her lover, part of the coup
- [[Elyas]] - Funded the coup attempt
