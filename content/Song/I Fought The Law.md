"I Fought the Law" by The Bobby Fuller Four played out during [[Darrow]]'s execution at the party's trial.

## Description

The song's inevitable conclusion "I fought the law and the law won" manifested when [[Ura]] (Arm of Law) beheaded [[Darrow]], splattering blood on the "LAW ONE" sign. Darrow had been sentenced to duel for his freedom after "shooting the sheriff."

## Connections

- [[Darrow]] - Beheaded by Ura after fighting the law
- [[Ura]] - Arm of Law who executed the sentence
- [[Material Plane Riots]] - Context of the trial

- LAW ONE sign - Splattered with Darrow's blood
