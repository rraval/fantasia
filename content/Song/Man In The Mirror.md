"Man in the Mirror" by Michael Jackson was referenced when the party discovered how to separate evil souls from bodies.

## Description

The song's theme of looking at oneself in the mirror connected to the party's discovery that they could separate evil souls from bodies and use those souls to power the [[Knife of Transposition]]. This breakthrough allowed them to create portals without sacrificing good people.

## Connections

- [[Knife of Transposition]] - Powered by separated evil souls
- [[Queen of Hearts]] - Provided evil souls (red caps) for the portals
- [[Transposition Event]] resolution - Used this method to return humans
- Red caps - Their evil souls were used after being separated from bodies
