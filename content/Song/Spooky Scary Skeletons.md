"Spooky Scary Skeletons" is referenced in [[Stodor]]'s backstory when skeletons rose from the ground.

## Description

During Stodor's tragic backstory, a party of explorers (including [[Hanzo]]) tumbled into the giant family's cave while fleeing from skeletons. The skeletons were rising from the ground, doubling on respawn. This undead wave was part of the attack triggered by the [[Synth Satellite]]'s bad moon.

## Connections

- [[Stodor]] - Skeletons attacked his family's cave
- [[Hanzo]] - Part of the explorer party fleeing skeletons
- [[Synth Satellite]] - Raised the waves of undead
- [[The Synth]] - Created the satellite
- Halloween session also referenced this song
