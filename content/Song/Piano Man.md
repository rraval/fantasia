"Piano Man" by Billy Joel is a signature song that represents [[Piano Man|the Piano Man]], ruler of the strings and bringer of the age of reason.

## Description

The song's themes of memory and storytelling connect deeply to the Piano Man's character. The line "Son can you play me a memory? I'm not really sure how it goes..." was sung by the Piano Man during his prophecy ritual.
