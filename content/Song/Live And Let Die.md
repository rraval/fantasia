"Live and Let Die" by Paul McCartney and Wings is the personal song of [[Cory]] and represents the corruption of the [[Queen of Hearts]]' original heraldry.

## Description

The Queen's actual heraldry is "LIVE AND LET LIVE," but during [[The Ball Massacre]], this was corrupted to "Live and Let Die" by red caps and the [[Cult of Darkness]]. When [[Vadu]] (Head of Law) was killed by red caps at the ball, it represented the influence of the [[Prince of Darkness]] on the Queen.
