"Mundiyan to bach ke" by Panjabi MC prominently features the [[Tumbi]], a traditional Punjabi stringed instrument.

## Description

When the party encountered half of the [[Piano Man]] playing a [[Tumbi]] inside the [[Knife of Transposition]], [[Velden]] demonstrated knowledge of the instrument by identifying this song as a famous track that prominently uses the Tumbi. The song blends traditional Punjabi folk music with modern bhangra beats, with the Tumbi providing its distinctive sound.
