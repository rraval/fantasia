"Against the Wind" by Bob Seger is a reflective song about looking back on younger days, which the [[Shadowfell]] wind spoke to the party as a warning during the clocktower battle.

## Description

During the clocktower confrontation with [[The Synth|the Fifth Prophet]], the whistling [[Shadowfell]] wind assembled a spoken warning over the course of the fight, drawing on this song's lyrics (see [[34 - Main 30]]). Fragments built up as the party made perception checks - "see the run," then "Janie was lovely," then "watch the young man run" - until, at full frenzy, it resolved into "watch the young man running against the wind."

The warning paid off at the end of the session: as retrieving a set of [[Pan Flute|pan pipes]] triggered a self-destruct explosion, [[Velden]] sprinted past the party, the "young man running against the wind."
