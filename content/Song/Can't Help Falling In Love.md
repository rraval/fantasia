"Can't Help Falling in Love" by Elvis Presley is connected to [[Rose]]'s tragic love story.

## Description

The song's themes of eternal, unavoidable love mirror Rose's devotion to [[Jack]]. Even after Jack's murder and her own death from grief, Rose's spirit couldn't help but continue loving him, waiting at the [[Love Shack]] until they could be reunited.

## Connections

- [[Rose]] - Ghost who died of grief after Jack's murder
- [[Jack]] - Her eternal love
- [[Love Shack]] - Where Rose waited as a ghost
- [[Dress of Love]] - Created from Rose's heart strings after finding peace
