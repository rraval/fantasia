"Little Drummer Boy" is a Christmas carol that inspired [[Drummer Boy]], a former orphan transformed into an undead avatar of [[Percussion]].

## Description

The song's "pa-ra-pa-pum" rhythm manifests literally in Drummer Boy's drumming. After being tortured by the [[Cult of Darkness]] and losing 8 fingers, he retained only his index fingers - which now serve as his drumsticks. He uses these two fingers to drum the "pa-ra-pa-pum" beat while commanding undead.
