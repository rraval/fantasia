"All Along the Watchtower" by Bob Dylan (famously covered by Jimi Hendrix) was quoted by [[Maurice]] when he first met the party.

## Description

The opening line "There must be some way out of here, said the joker to the thief" connects to Maurice's identity as The Joker and his desire to escape the [[Cantum cycle]].

## Connections

- [[Maurice]] - Quoted this song when meeting the party, looking at [[Cory]] (a rogue)
- Reflects Maurice's quest to find escape from immortality
