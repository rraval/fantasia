"Bring Sally Up" (originally "Flower" by Moby, popularized as a workout song) inspired the "Sorcery Assisted Linear Lift (Yellow)" - abbreviated as SALLY.

## Description

The party found a lever that raised the ball return floor to reveal an elevator with this acronym referencing the song's famous lyric "bring sally up, bring sally down."
