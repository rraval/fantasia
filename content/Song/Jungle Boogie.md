"Jungle Boogie" by Kool & the Gang was referenced when the party returned to [[Lyric]] after it had been transposed with jungle elements.

## Description

After the [[Transposition Event]], Lyric was overrun by jungle. The song's famous line "Get down, gotta get down" was quoted by a human trapped upside down in a web during this jungle-transformed version of Lyric.
