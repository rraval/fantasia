"The Gambler" by Kenny Rogers is another alias and thematic connection for [[Maurice]].

## Description

The song's wisdom about "knowing when to hold 'em, fold 'em, walk away, and run" reflects Maurice's ancient knowledge and strategic thinking. He quoted this song when revealing he's been alive a long time and "bet on many parties before."

## Connections

- [[Maurice]] - Also known as "The Gambler"
- Quoted on train to [[Paradise City]]
- Reflects Maurice's long existence and strategic nature
