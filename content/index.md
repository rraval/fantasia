Fantasia is a D&D 5e campaign inspired by western pop music. Season 1 played out over 27 mainline sessions and 4 one shots from spring 2023 - spring 2024. Season 2 is imminent.

## Catch Up

Newly crowned champions of [[The Intervals]], the party finds itself entagled in a coup with [[Amras]], the [[Uptown Girl]], and the elves of [[Elyas]] against the [[Piano Man]] (aka Man of Strings). The Piano Man "escapes" by stabbing himself with the [[Knife of Transposition]], which captures his soul. Diverting the usurpers with the [[Dress of Love]], the party buys a few hours to rest.

They wake floating in in a [[Planes#Orchestral|giant orchestra hall]], with 2 strong and 1 weak ethereal bindings from each PC leading off to the distance. The first strong binding leads to the percussion section, with 4 subsections out of 7 total in shambles. The second strong binding leads to the conductor section, which has a large pile of goblin bodies with various written notes on the bodies. The last weak binding leads to the strings section, where a faint apparition of the Piano Man wanders with incoherent ramblings and no memory of recent events.

Interrupted from their dream, the same tricks no longer work with the guards. The party escapes [[Tensegrity]] to find [[a budding revolution]] amidst the [[Paradise City|disintegrating city]]. [[Maurice]] driving a caravan gives them a ride, and trades a secret for a secret to reveal that the PCs are not from this verse. He offers a deal: smash the [[Forces|instruments]], destroy this verse, free him from his [[endless cycle]], and the PCs can return to their home verses.

## Mechanics

- Transposition: a form of interplanar travel. Time passes differently between planes by a factor of 2x, e.g. 1 second on the material plane is 2 seconds in the Feywild. See [[Knife of Transposition]] and [[instrument panel]].
- Dress of Love: makes creatures believe the wearer is "the person they love the most". Seems to be powered by moonlight and subsequent uses seem less effective.
- Moon Orb: Reservoir of moonlight. Can act as a lens.
