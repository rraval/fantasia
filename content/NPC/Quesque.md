Quesque is a mysterious merchant who repeatedly appears at Feywild markets selling apples from the material plane.

## Description

- Recurring merchant at Feywild markets
- Sells apples at 2gp each
- Apples are from the material plane (unusual in Feywild)
- Does not explain products or methods very well
- Refuses to answer questions about planar travel capabilities
- Mysterious and evasive about origins

## Items

- **Apples from the Material Plane** - 2gp each
  - Significant given the plane separation and transposition crisis
  - Suggests access to planar travel or interdimensional trade routes
  - Priced as luxury/rare items in the Feywild

## Chronology

### First Night Market Encounter

- First appearance at a night market
- Offering apples at 2gp each
- Did not explain offerings very well
- Present during the [[Psycho Killer]] incident where a hooded figure captured a woman and drained her into a ruby

### Feywild Market Encounter

- Party encountered Quesque at the market again
- Tried to sell them apples from the material plane
- Party attempted to learn more about his planar travel methods
- Quesque shut down all inquiries, refusing to explain
- Party left frustrated by his evasiveness

### Halloween Clue

- During [[Stodor]]'s storytelling at the [[Bally Table]]
- A clue in the cauldron read: "Buy Quesque's apples!"
