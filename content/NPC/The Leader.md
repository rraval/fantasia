The Leader is a self-styled criminal who attempted to seize control during the [[Material Plane Riots]] in [[Lyric]], ultimately meeting his downfall in the Duke's mansion.

## Description

- Small-time figure who styled himself as "The Leader"
- New to position of leadership, not respected by his minions
- Attempted to establish "new world order"
- In way over his head
- Died fighting the party in the Duke's mansion

## Chronology

### Courthouse Interruption

- Interrupted the trial of party members with 3 minions
- Claimed something about a "new world order"
- Party perceived him as new to the job, not entirely respected by minions
- Leader and minion A fought [[Ura]] (the Arm) trying to get to [[Vadu]] (the Head)
- Minion B freed the party from chains
- Minion C went upstairs to ransack the courthouse
- After party was freed, Leader disengaged from combat
- Tried to convince party to follow him with "Follow the Leader"
- Party refused his recruitment attempt

### Aftermath and Reputation

- One of his minions (baker's apprentice) burnt down the courthouse
- The Duke of Lyric was killed, the mansion ransacked (likely connected to The Leader's operations)
- [[Viscount Bridgerton]] dismissed him as "a small time figure not worthy of attention"
- [[Sam]] got mixed in with The Leader "because he seemed in charge"

### The Duke's Mansion Standoff

- [[Ura]] came to the party for help at the [[Tinkers Guild]]
- Ura was trying to lead the people of Lyric
- The Leader tried to seize interim control and was taking her presence poorly
- **The Leader poisoned the water supply**
- Seized the only antidote and retreated into the Duke's mansion
- Set off numerous traps in the mansion
- Ura lost 12 "red shirts" (followers) trying to storm the mansion directly

- Party infiltrated the Duke's mansion via the roof
- Found The Leader and 3 voices arguing angrily behind a collapsed dome
- Parts of the mansion were replaced with jungle from transposition
- The Leader had triggered traps and angered jungle monsters
- [[Louis Orrai]] used Detect Thoughts and discovered The Leader was "in way over his head"

### The Final Confrontation:
- Party triggered webs, which they set on fire
- Fire triggered brown mold expansion
- Alerted phase spider and spider swarms
- During the spider combat, The Leader and his thugs showed up
- Offered health potions to help the party... at a price
- Party accepted their help then immediately double-crossed them
- Combat initiated: The Leader (Bandit Captain) + 2 Thugs + 3 Mastiffs
- The Leader was killed in the fighting

### Aftermath

- [[Sam]] explained to the party how he got involved with The Leader
- Sam had been searching for the party in Lyric
- Got mixed in with The Leader "because he seemed in charge"

## Musical Themes

The Leader's story featured "Follow the Leader" when he tried to recruit the party.

## Connections

- Opposed [[Ura]] for control of post-riot Lyric
- Had multiple minions including:
  - Baker's apprentice who burned the courthouse
  - Various thugs and commoners
  - 3 Mastiffs
- [[Sam]] briefly associated with him during chaos
- Killed the lord of Lyric and ransacked the manor
- Dismissed by [[Viscount Bridgerton]] as insignificant
- Died at the hands of the party in the Duke's mansion
