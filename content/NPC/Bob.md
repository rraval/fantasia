Bob is a wizard who was imprisoned in the bumpers of the [[Bally Table]] pinball machine and rescued by the party.

## Description

- Wizard
- Was trapped inside the bumpers/jails of the [[Bally Table]] pinball machine
- One of two prisoners (the other was a warrior)
- Rescued by the party

## Imprisonment

Bob was one of two prisoners held in the bumpers of the [[Bally Table]] pinball machine:
- The bumpers appeared to be jails holding captives
- Bob was in one bumper, a warrior in the other
- Opening one jail would cause the other to be incinerated
- This created a moral dilemma for rescuers
- The party chose to rescue Bob

## Chronology

### Rescue from Bally Table

- Trapped in [[Bally Table]] pinball machine at top of mountain
- Party discovered the bumpers were actually jails
- Faced with choice: save Bob or save the warrior
- Party rescued Bob
- The warrior in the other bumper was incinerated

### Escape

- Traveled with the party after rescue
- May have triggered the Jump puzzle (or so [[Cory]] claims)
- Left the Bally Table facility with the party

## Connection to Stodor

Bob and [[Stodor]] were both prisoners at the [[Bally Table]]:
- Stodor was chained to the machine, used to throw pinballs
- Bob was trapped inside the machine, held in a bumper/jail
- Both were victims of [[Viscount Bridgerton]]'s operation
- However, they have separate backstories:
  - Stodor was captured by [[The Synth]] after destroying a [[Synth Satellite]]
  - Bob's capture circumstances are unknown
