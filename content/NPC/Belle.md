Belle (also called Terribelle or "The Belle") is the ancient avatar of Percussion who once ruled over all 7 planes before being overthrown by [[Piano Man|Man of Strings]].

## Description

- Represents the [[Forces#Percussion|Percussion]] force (drums of war)
- Associated with violence, conquest, and warfare

## History

As explained by the [[Queen of Hearts]].

### Age of the Belle

- Ruled over all 7 [[Planar System|planes]] of existence
- "Ravaged the land" during their reign
- Contact between planes was common during this era
- Reign characterized by warfare and conquest
- Held power through the Percussion force

### The Overthrow

- [[Piano Man|Man of Strings]] united a "seven nation army" to resist Belle
- Organized rebellion across all planes
- Belle's influence was eradicated from all 7 planes of existence
- Each nation/plane was given a [[Chords of Power|Chord of Power]] after the victory
- This marked the end of the Age of Belle and beginning of the Age of Reason

### After the Fall

- Contact between planes ceased for hundreds of years
- The age of reason with diplomacy and cooperation replaced warfare
- Belle's influence remained minimal until recently
- [[Tensegrity]] established with presence in every plane

## Connections

- Opposed and overthrown by [[Piano Man]]
- Connected to Percussion force and drums of war
- Associated with artifacts like [[Chords of Power]]
- Historical connection to the planes being interconnected
