The Synth is an antagonist faction founded by [[Maurice]] but which has since separated in vision.

## Description

- Organization/faction rather than individual
- Founded by [[Maurice]] but now operating independently with different goals
- Led by someone called the "Fifth Prophet"
- Associated with modern/synthetic elements and advanced technology
- Connected to dwarfs in Hall of the Mountain King

## Technology and Projects

**Synth Satellites:**
- Create artificial moons that raise undead
- Golden-red in color
- One satellite was destroyed by [[Stodor]] during the Halloween incident
- Used to experiment with necromancy and mass resurrection

**Mechanical Augmentation:**
- Funded [[Viscount Bridgerton]]'s armored mech suit
- The mech mentioned "a deal fulfilled" before self-destructing
- Likely providing technology to various agents

**The Bally Table:**
- Captured [[Stodor]] and chained him to the [[Bally Table]] pinball machine
- Used him as a living component after he destroyed their satellite

## Activities

- Assassination attempts on important figures
- Attacking competitors during [[The Intervals]]
- Deploying experimental weapons (Synth Satellites)
- Funding technological projects and agents
- Capturing and enslaving powerful beings
- Operating across multiple planes

## Chronology

### The Synth Satellite

- Deployed a Synth Satellite disguised as an unnatural golden-red moon
- The satellite raised waves of undead (Ogre Zombie, Beholder Zombie, Revenant, Wraith)
- [[Stodor]]'s family was killed in the resulting battle
- Stodor threw a boulder that cracked and destroyed the satellite
- The Synth traced the trail of stones back to Stodor
- Captured him and enslaved him to the [[Bally Table]]

### Viscount Bridgerton's Mech

- [[Viscount Bridgerton]] fought the party in a mech suit at the [[Bally Table]]
- Party damaged the mech by throwing grenades back at it
- The mech made mention of "a deal fulfilled" before self-destructing
- Killed the Viscount in the explosion
- Revealed The Synth's involvement in funding the operation

### Train Assassination Attempt

- Attempted to assassinate [[Augur of Melodies]] on train to [[Paradise City]]
- Used doppelganger (disguised as maid) and assassin (disguised as bartender)
- Knocked out guards and VIP with gas attack
- Party stopped the assassination
- As train approached bridge, a Neothelid attacked the train
- [[Ragnar Rok]] claimed credit for killing the creature

### Dismissed by Piano Man

- [[Piano Man]] thanked party for saving the Augur
- Dismissed [[The Synth]] as merely an "annoyance"
- Implied they're not a serious threat to his power
- This may be dangerous underestimation given their actual capabilities

### The Intervals Attacks

**Inn Ambush:**
- Assassins from The Synth ambushed the inn where party was staying
- **The Theremin Twins** attacked:
  - Mist creature that turned into a werebear
  - Yochlol strumming a theremin
- Specifically targeted [[Helia Oriela]]
- Party defeated them quickly with moonbeam

**Revelations:**
- The Synth is led by the "Fifth Prophet"
- They were attacking all teams competing in The Intervals
- [[My Anaconda Don't]] (Yuan-Ti team) was killed off and ravaged by The Synth

### Maurice's Revelation

- [[Maurice]] revealed he founded The Synth
- They have since separated in vision from Maurice
- Maurice seeks escape from the Cantum cycle

## Connections

- Founded by [[Maurice]] but now pursuing different goals
- Funded [[Viscount Bridgerton]]'s mech suit and [[Bally Table]] operation
- Captured and enslaved [[Stodor]] after he destroyed their satellite
- Opposed by [[Piano Man]] (who underestimates them)
- Attacking prophets like [[Augur of Melodies]]
- Targeted [[The Intervals]] competitors
- Creating Synth Satellites for necromantic experiments
