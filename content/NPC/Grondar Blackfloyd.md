Grondar Blackfloyd is a dwarf guard at [[Paradise City]] and cousin to [[Ragnar Rok]].

## Description

- Dwarf guard/military
- Works for or with forces of [[Piano Man|Man of Strings]]
- Related to [[Ragnar Rok]] (cousins)

## Chronology

### Train Arrival

- Party explained what happened on train ride to him:
  - Maid and bartender were assassins
  - They gassed the guards and VIP
  - Guards are dead, but VIP ([[Augur of Melodies]]) was revived by [[Helia Oriela]]
- Asked what happened to the assassins - no bodies could be found
- Asked about Neothelid attack
- When guards poked Neothelid corpse, [[Ragnar Rok]] rolled out claiming to have killed it
- Ragnar exclaimed "COUSIN" upon seeing Grondar
- Grondar recognized Ragnar and facepalmed
- Escorted party and [[Augur of Melodies]] toward [[Tensegrity]]

### Journey to Tensegrity
- As party passed through [[Paradise City]], they noticed blemishes, cracks, and buildings falling apart
- Many creatures huddled around garbage fires
- Arrived at [[Tensegrity]], massive structure with spikes sticking out of ground, suspended by strings

### Revolution

- Taken by party when they jumped out window with pass without trace
- Train revolution was happening at the time
- Accompanied party with [[Ragnar Rok]]

## Relationships

- Cousin to [[Ragnar Rok]] (clearly embarrassed by the relation)
- Works with guards loyal to [[Piano Man]]
- Professional and dutiful despite cousin's antics
