David Blane is a halfling who repeatedly ran away from his own wedding, eventually married after the party's intervention.

## Description

- Halfling male
- Prone to self-doubt and running away
- Has three brothers who keep trying to get him married
- Depressed and uncertain about commitment

## Items

- Had "The Blane Map" which party received as reward

## Chronology

### First Encounter

- Party encountered him dressed as a halfling woman
- Had been crying and was not sure of himself
- Asked to travel with party for company
- Asked party to say he is "Dylan's wife" if anyone asks
- Party was divided on helping him

### Meeting His Brothers
- Three halfling brothers appeared, asking about David
- Revealed David was supposed to get married that day
- This was the third time David had run away from his wedding
- David was depressed: "What if I'm wrong" about signing bride up for life with him
- [[Harloc]] was concerned and ensured David really wanted to marry

### The Grove Wedding
- Party arrived at [[The Grove]] for wedding
- During ceremony, cart overturned revealing 2 goblins and goblin boss
- Goblins tried to scare halflings
- Party fought off goblins with entangle and sleep spells
- 2 more goblins appeared and hustled David away to goblin lair

### Rescue
- Party tracked David to abandoned mine
- [[Cory]] went ahead, triggered trapped beam causing rockfall
- Found David tied up and crying
- David had enlisted the Goblins to kidnap him from his wedding
- Cory slapped David hard enough to make him bleed from temple (4 damage)
- Party dug out of rockfall (took several hours)
- Goblin boss had escaped but 2 goblins still tied up

### Shotgun Wedding
- Party left David tied up
- Returned quickly to The Grove
- [[Cory]] officiated shotgun wedding
- Party received [[The Blane Map]] as reward for returning and marrying David
- Party warned halflings about potential goblin attack

## Connections

- Three brothers trying to get him married
- Paid goblin raiders who betrayed him
- [[Harloc]] showed particular concern for his wellbeing
- [[Cory]] took harsh approach to "help" him commit
