Amras is a cleric/healer who initially served [[Piano Man]] but later joined [[Uptown Girl]] in a coup attempt against him.

## Abilities

- Healing magic and curse detection
- Can diagnose complex magical curses like [[Feeblemind]]
- Knowledge of potions and cures
- Each feeblemind has a different cure, and Amras knew how to find [[Velden]]'s cure

## Relationships

- Works with [[Uptown Girl]] in coup against [[Piano Man]]
- Funded by [[Elyas]]
- Encountered the party multiple times
- Initially served [[Piano Man]], bringing him [[Tonic]]

## Chronology

### Meeting at Tensegrity

- Waited at bottom of elevator entrance to [[Tensegrity]]
- Holding a [[Tonic]] giving off faint blue luminescence
- Escorted up with party by [[Uptown Girl]]
- Examined [[Velden]] in the elevator
- Revealed [[Velden]] had been cursed with [[Feeblemind]]
- Explained the curse reverted Velden's core stats to base instincts only
- Offered a potion to lead party to the cure
- Initially asked for a future favor as payment (creating debt/obligation)
- [[Cory]] and [[Harloc]] tricked him with a palmed coin toss (double-or-nothing)
- Didn't notice the trick, party got the potion for free
- Presented [[Tonic]] to [[Piano Man]], who underwent physical transformation

### After the Coup Attempt

- [[The Coup|Coup attempt]] failed when [[Piano Man]] stabbed himself with [[Knife of Transposition]]
- Piano Man intentionally trapped his own soul, denying them their prize
- Retreated to Uptown with [[Uptown Girl]]
- [[Vadu]] was killed during this tumultuous period
- Saying "the wildest things that there is no Elyas here"
- Trying to maintain cover for the coup conspiracy
