The "Princess of Diamonds" was actually a Green Hag named Bonnie in disguise who tricked the party into helping her before being discovered and killed.

## Description

- Appeared as beautiful young woman/princess
- Actually a Green Hag using illusion magic
- Not well regarded by fae messengers
- Had ancestral cave holdings

## Backstory: The Seamstress

Before her time as the "Princess of Diamonds", Bonnie lived as a seamstress at the [[Love Shack]] with a water mill and loom. This cottage became the site of a tragic love triangle:

- Bonnie fell in love with [[Jack]], a local man
- Jack instead fell in love with [[Rose]], daughter of [[DeWitt]]
- Consumed by jealousy, Bonnie orchestrated a deadly deception:
  - Lied to DeWitt, telling him Jack was a vampire
  - Lied to Jack, telling him Rose would meet him at [[Wise Men Falls]]
  - DeWitt killed Jack with a crossbow bolt through the heart
  - Rose died of grief, becoming a ghost
- After Rose's spirit found peace, she possessed the water wheel at the cottage
- Rose's heart strings fed the loom, producing a single bolt of moonlight cloth
- Bonnie took this magical cloth: the [[Dress of Love]]

This backstory was depicted in a drama performed at an inn during [[The Intervals]], explaining the origin of the Dress of Love.

## The Deception

Claimed to be:
- Princess of Diamonds in love with Ace of Spades
- Victim of [[Queen of Hearts]]' power grab
- Targeted for execution unjustly

Reality:
- Green Hag using disguise magic
- Attempting to manipulate party
- Poorly regarded by actual fae ("that creature deserved what came to her")

## Chronology

### The Rescue

- Three satyrs dragging her as prisoner
- She pleaded for life, mentioning their prince
- Party intervened from cliff vantage
- Combat with satyrs - party killed 2, one escaped
- Found royal writ of execution on satyr captain

### Her Story (lies)
- Claimed to be Princess of Diamonds
- Said she was in love with Ace of Spades
- Claimed [[Queen of Hearts]] seized control
- Said she was in small town when invaded by humans
- Humans appeared 4 days ago (different timeline than party's)
- No jungle transported out from her location
- Queen allegedly had her kidnapped for execution
- Couldn't contact Ace of Spades

### Journey to Town
- Party approached town with her
- Remains of [[Lyric]] literally transposed in patches
- Large military presence, humans being corralled
- [[Helia Oriela]] questioned messenger who revealed:
  - Town seized under Ace of Spades' orders
  - Queen of Hearts acting as emissary
  - No one had seen Ace of Spades since humans arrived
  - "Princess of diamonds is not well regarded, that creature deserved what came to her"

### Ancestral Cave
- Suggested retreating to her ancestral holdings
- Old cave with minor furnishes and overgrown mold
- Party spent hours arguing about goals

### Political Explanations
- House of clubs in disrepair after events centuries ago
- Queen of Hearts was consort to King, clutching at lost power
- Ace of Spades has most current power
- Claimed Ace loved her, lifting her house above squalor
- Party sensed she wasn't entirely truthful but couldn't pinpoint lies

### Discovery and Death

- [[Helia Oriela]] confronted her about lying
- Princess was dismayed
- Revealed true form as Green Hag
- Combat initiated
- Party dispatched the hag

## Loot

- 5 vials: 3 empty, 2 yellowish with floating eyeballs (potions of clairvoyance)
