Sam is a bartender from [[Ode]] who helped the party by caring for orphan children and providing information.

## Background

- Bartender in [[Ode]]
- Good-hearted person willing to help
- Got caught up in events with [[The Leader]] during riots

## Chronology

### Taking Care of Children

- Party brought orphan children (including [[Drummer Boy]]) to [[Ode]]
- Sam agreed to care for the children
- [[Hanzo]] considered leaving the party after delivering children to Sam

### Reunion and Information

- Caught up with party
- Came to [[Lyric]] searching for them
- Got mixed in with [[The Leader]] "because he seemed in charge"
- Provided crucial information about [[Viscount Bridgerton]]:
  - Viscount was very upset
  - Made local dealings
  - Handed off children to [[Cult of Darkness]] mine
- Traveled with party to the mine near [[Ode]]
- Waited outside mine with donkey while party entered
- Witnessed aftermath of [[Drummer Boy]] encounter
