Vadu was the Head of Law in the first judicial office in [[Lyric]], responsible for hearing pleas and determining guilt. Brother of [[Elyas]].

## Role

- Head of Law in first judicial office of Lyric
- Worked alongside [[Ura]] (Arm of Law) who declared atonements
- Judge who heard cases and determined guilt

## Chronology

### Party's Trial

- Presided over party's trial in courthouse
- Gestured at massive "LAW ONE" sign on wall
- Explained the treaty of chants giving defendants right to defend themselves
- Heard pleas and accusations for each party member:
  - [[Hanzo]]: unlicensed food wagon and vandalism
  - [[Harloc]]: unspecified charges
  - [[Cory]]: embezzlement of Trock company
  - [[Ev]]: unspecified charges
  - [[Darrow]]: shooting the sheriff
  - [[Helia Oriela]]: arson and inciting mutiny
- Trial interrupted by [[The Leader]] and minions
- [[The Leader]] and minion fought trying to get to Vadu

### Death at Queen's Ball

- [[Queen of Hearts]] brought Vadu to her ball
- Made speech about "live and let live" turned "live and let die"
- [[8 of spades]] raised toast
- Red cap decapitated 8 of spades
- Combat erupted
- Vadu (Head of Law) was killed by red caps during the massacre

### Aftermath

- Guards found [[Ura]] trying to escape town of diamonds
- Ura asked about Vadu
- Party told her Vadu was dead
- Ura hung her head in shame

### Confirmed Death

- Guards confirmed Vadu had "definitely perished" at [[Tensegrity]]
- [[Uptown Girl]] and [[Amras]] retreated to Uptown after Vadu's death
