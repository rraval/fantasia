Ragnar Rok is a swarmy mercenary orc with a Cockney accent, eager to claim credit and gold. He is cousin to [[Grondar Blackfloyd]].

## Description

- Orc mercenary
- Swarmy personality with Cockney accent
- Opportunistic and eager for credit
- Always looking for profit

## Personality

- Claims credit for others' achievements
- Showboating and attention-seeking
- Mercenary mindset focused on gold
- Despite flaws, occasionally helpful

## Chronology

### Monster of the Deep

- Party killed Hungry Sorrowsworn
- Party killed Neothelid on train
- When guards poked Neothelid corpse, Ragnar rolled out from inside/under it
- Claimed to have killed the Neothelid himself (stealing party's credit)
- Party initially denied his claim
- Saw [[Grondar Blackfloyd]] and exclaimed "COUSIN"
- Grondar facepalmed upon recognizing him

### Desert Rose Quest

- Part of the party with [[Velden]], [[Cory]], [[Ev]], [[Harloc]], and [[Louis Orrai]]
- Participated in breaking [[Velden]]'s feeblemind curse
- Fought Kruthiks in the desert

### Revolution

- Taken along when party jumped out window
- Accompanied party with [[Grondar Blackfloyd]]
- Present during train revolution

## Relationships

- Cousin to [[Grondar Blackfloyd]] (who is embarrassed by him)
- Travels with party opportunistically
- Attempts to claim credit for their achievements
