Uptown Girl is a leader of the coup attempt against [[Piano Man]], working alongside [[Amras]] and funded by [[Elyas]].

## Description

- Haughty demeanor
- In a position of authority at [[Tensegrity]]

## Relationships

- Romantic partner of the [[Piano Man]], but has an affair with [[Amras]]
- Connected to [[Elyas]] and the rest of his elves
- Initially served [[Piano Man]] before turning against him

## Chronology

### Meeting at Tensegrity

- Opened elevator to take [[Amras]] and party up to [[Piano Man]]'s chamber
- Exclaimed at [[Augur of Melodies]]' appearance
- Questioned party briefly
- Then haughtily ignored them
- Escorted everyone up to Piano Man

### After the Coup Attempt

- [[The Coup|Coup attempt]] failed when [[Piano Man]] stabbed himself with [[Knife of Transposition]]
- Piano Man denied them their prize by trapping his own soul
- [[Vadu]] (Head of Law) was killed during this tumultuous period
- Uptown Girl and [[Amras]] retreated to Uptown
- Saying "the wildest things that there is no Elyas here"
- Trying to deny connection to [[Elyas]] and the coup conspiracy
- Guards informed party that Vadu had "definitely perished"
