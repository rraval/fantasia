Jack was a man who fell in love with [[Rose]], but was murdered by her father [[DeWitt]] due to [[Princess of Diamonds|Bonnie]]'s jealous deception.

## Description

- In love with [[Rose]], daughter of [[DeWitt]]
- Murdered at [[Wise Men Falls]]
- Became a spirit trapped at the falls
- Body stuck at the bottom of the lake with a crossbow bolt through his heart

## Story

Jack's love for Rose ended in tragedy:

- [[Princess of Diamonds|Bonnie]], the seamstress, loved Jack first
- Jack chose [[Rose]] instead, sparking Bonnie's jealousy
- Bonnie orchestrated a deadly deception:
  - Told DeWitt that Jack was a vampire
  - Told Jack that Rose would meet him at [[Wise Men Falls]]
- DeWitt ambushed Jack at the falls
- Killed him with a crossbow bolt through the heart
- Jack's body fell into the lake

## Wise Men Falls

After his death, Jack's spirit remained at [[Wise Men Falls]]:

- The waterfall stopped flowing, the lake became blocked
- Jack's spirit haunted the misty falls
- Tried to convince visitors to jump into the lake
- Unwilling to move on without [[Rose]]
- His body remained trapped at the bottom

## Finding Peace

The party eventually helped Jack find peace:

- Required DEX saves to avoid falling into the lake
- Had to retrieve Jack's body from the bottom
- With the body recovered, the lake unblocked
- The waterfall activated again
- Jack's spirit could finally move on after reuniting with Rose's spirit

## The Dress of Love

Jack's death, along with Rose's grief, ultimately led to the creation of the [[Dress of Love]]:

- After learning the truth, [[Rose]] found peace
- Rose possessed the water wheel at the seamstress cottage
- Her heart strings fed the loom, producing the magical cloth

## Musical Themes

Jack's story was associated with the song "Can't Help Falling in Love":
- "Wise men say, only fools rush in"
- "Like a river flows, surely to the sea, darling so it goes, some things are meant to be"
- The location [[Wise Men Falls]] references this song
