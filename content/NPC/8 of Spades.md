8 of Spades was a member of the fae court who attempted to give a toast at [[Queen of Hearts]]' ball before being decapitated by a red cap.

## Chronology

### The Ball Massacre

- Attended [[Queen of Hearts]]' ball
- Raised a toast
- Started to speak
- Red cap decapitated him mid-toast, demanding "Blood"
- His death triggered the combat at the ball
