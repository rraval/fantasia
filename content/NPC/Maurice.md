Maurice, also known as "The Joker", is an immortal conductor who bound the [[Cantum cycle]]. He is a central figure in the campaign, seeking escape from his immortality. He wields the [[Knife of Transposition]], a powerful dagger that can open portals between planes.

## Description

- Changes faces and appearances
- Smokes and blows smoke rings (12 rings at midnight - an auspicious number)
- Loves "all things interesting" and himself most of all
- Only plays his music in the sun
- Wanted by city guard with "capture on sight" orders for unspecified "sins"

## Abilities

- Can change his appearance by dying and taking possession of another Goblin's body
- Wields the [[Knife of Transposition]]
- Powerful magic user (cast spells that make enemies double over laughing)
- Immortal, bound to the [[Cantum cycle]]
- Has been alive a long time and "bet on many parties before"

## Goals

- Seeking escape from his immortality
- Wants the party to destroy the instruments to break the cycle
- Offered deal: "Kill the instruments, smash them, break them, free me from the cycle. You can go back to your own verses."

## Relationships

- Founded [[The Synth]], but they have since separated in vision
- Knows about [[Drummer Boy]] and has been tracking the party
- Took a particular liking to [[Harloc]], surprised he hasn't been "claimed yet"

## Chronology

### Meeting the Party

- Met the party at a crossroads outside [[Lyric]] during [[Material Plane Riots]]
- Mentioned "You cannot hear the drums in your head? Why do you think the city is on fire!"
- Asked [[Harloc]] if he's seen lots of rats recently
- Joined the party
- Quoted [[All Along The Watchtower|"All Along the Watchtower"]]: "There must be some way out of here, said the joker to the thief" (looking at [[Cory]])
- Wanted to taste the Meenlock meat
- Cackled as party escaped through the rusted gate

### Staged Death

- Morning after escaping Lyric, children found Maurice "dead" with his own dagger in his chest
- Left a note: "GOTTA BE ELSEWHERE. DON'T YOU WORRY, I'M RIGHT HERE AT HOME"
- This happened the same morning as the [[Transposition Event]]
- Party took his [[Knife of Transposition]] and lute
- Maurice later appeared alive on train to [[Paradise City]]

### Feywild

- Party used Maurice's knife ([[Knife of Transposition]]) to demonstrate portal creation to [[Queen of Hearts]]
- [[Hanzo]] wielded the knife, carved a tear in reality using a gem
- The gem magically fused with the hilt, feeding energy into the portal
- Portal accidentally sliced through a person on the other side, revealing [[Ev]]
- Time moves twice as fast in Feywild compared to Material Plane
- The inch-long gem was expended in about 2 minutes of keeping portal open

### Paradise City

- Encountered party on train to [[Paradise City]], wearing a different face
- Revealed he knows party has encountered [[Drummer Boy]]
- Cackled about being alive a long time and betting on many parties
- Mentioned "there's a prophecy afoot"
- Quoted [[The Gambler|"The Gambler"]]: knowledge of when to hold 'em, fold 'em, walk away, and run
- Jumped out the train window

### Revelations

- Very happy that [[Piano Man|Man of Strings]] had been captured
- Traded secrets with the party:
  - [[Helia]]'s secret: "She knew that she was guilty when she plead not guilty"
  - Maurice's secret: "Speaking of that trial: do you ever wonder why you have so few memories of this place? Why you know so little? The [[War Against Hippity-Hops]] did happen!"
- Revealed party members are not from this verse
- Made his deal: destroy the instruments to break the cycle and return home
- Confirmed he is the conductor who bound the [[Cantum cycle]]
- Confirmed he founded [[The Synth]] but they've since diverged

### Escape from [[Paradise City]]

- Drove wagon giving party escape from collapsing [[Tensegrity]]
- Party in back of wagon as [[Paradise City]] disintegrated
- [[Louis Orrai]] noticed the [[Knife of Transposition]] glowing
- Louis examined the dagger
- Party suddenly pulled into the knife itself
