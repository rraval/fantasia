Prince of Darkness refers to three priests of a relatively modern "religion of darkness" or "Religion of Minism" who tortured children and were involved in dark rituals.

## Description

- Three priests following their own whims
- Part of relatively modern religion with central organizer
- Have distinctive tattoos
- Associated with darkness and shadow magic
- Consider darkness sacred (view it as blasphemy to dispel)

## The Three Priests

1. **Priest at Queen's Ball** - Accompanied [[Queen of Hearts]], killed by party
2. **Mage in the Mine** - Had claw familiar and ioun stone, contracted by [[Viscount Bridgerton]]
3. **Third Priest** - Status unknown

## Abilities

- Dark magic and shadow powers
- Ability to influence others (influenced Queen of Hearts temporarily)
- Counter spells and combat magic
- Tattoos that interact with darkness magic

## Chronology

### The Ball

- One priest accompanied [[Queen of Hearts]] to the ball
- Queen had been changed to red livery, appeared influenced
- When priest was knocked unconscious then killed:
  - First reflection shattered
  - Second reflection zipped around priest then shattered
  - Third reflection zipped around priest and went into the body
- Body was taken for examination

### Body Examination

- Party investigated the priest's body with servants
- Found a small pile of bodies
- Exposing body to sunlight revealed waves of darkness emanating from veins
- Darkness was held at bay by tattoos
- Body exploded in blinding light dealing damage

### Mine Discovery

- Party found grotesque remains of priests and workers in [[Cult of Darkness]] mine
- All had tattoos similar to Prince of Darkness from Feywild
- Bodies were pinned to the wall
- [[Drummer Boy]] had killed them

### Cult Mage

- Party encountered mage with similar tattoos in the mine
- Had claw familiar and ioun stone (Intellect)
- Revealed religion details:
  - Three priests that follow their own whims
  - Relatively modern religion with central organizer
  - Only contracting with [[Viscount Bridgerton]] after he was humiliated
  - Weren't intending to torture, merely extract information about party
- [[Hanzo]] cast darkness, mage considered it blasphemy
- Party killed the mage and his companions
- Mage was very attached to his claw familiar

## Connections

- Supported by [[Viscount Bridgerton]]
- Tortured children in mine near [[Ode]]
- Temporarily influenced [[Queen of Hearts]]
- Connected to [[Drummer Boy]]'s transformation
- Killed by party and by Drummer Boy
