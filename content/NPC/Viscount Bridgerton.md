Viscount Bridgerton is a corrupt noble who sold orphan children to the [[Cult of Darkness]], leading to their torture and the transformation of [[Drummer Boy]].

## Description

- Noble with title of Viscount
- Operated near [[Ode]]
- Had saddle bags and rooms that party searched

## Actions

- Sold orphan children to [[Cult of Darkness]] mine
- Made local dealings after being "very upset" about something
- His actions led to children being tortured by the cult

## Chronology

### Discovery

- Party stole [[Ciphered Note in Ode]] from Viscount's saddle bags
- Searched his rooms, found chest containing [[Pan Flute]]
- Pan Flute was rigged with gem stones, lenses, and mechanical contraptions
- Not intrinsically magical, but resonates with magic
- Blowing into the flute did nothing - holes appeared blocked

### Consequences Revealed

- [[Sam]] caught up with party and revealed:
  - Viscount Bridgerton was very upset (about party's interference)
  - Made local dealings and handed off children to the mine cult
  - Children were taken to [[Cult of Darkness]] mine near [[Ode]]
- Party traveled to mine and found the horrific results
- Children had been tortured and transformed into undead
- [[Drummer Boy]] blamed party for "not being there to save them"

### Cult Connection

- Party encountered [[Prince of Darkness]] mage in the mine
- Mage revealed they were only contracting after "the viscount was humiliated"
- They weren't intending to torture, merely extract information
- Viscount was looking for the party

## Connections

- Connected to [[Cult of Darkness]] / Religion of Minism
- Sold children including [[Drummer Boy]] to the cult
- Had [[Pan Flute]], a key instrument in transposition events
- Had dealings with [[The Synth]], may have been indebted to them
