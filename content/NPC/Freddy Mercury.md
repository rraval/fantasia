Freddy Mercury, also known as "The Champions", is actually a Couatl (celestial serpent) disguised as a human who competed in [[The Intervals]].

## Description

- Appears as human but is actually a Couatl
- Competed in [[The Intervals]] tournament
- Associated with the concept of champions
- Aware of the [[Cantum cycle]], mocks [[Velden]] for still believing in his god.

## True Nature

- Couatl - celestial winged serpent creature
- Capable of disguising as human
- Powerful celestial being

## Chronology

### The Intervals

- Competed in [[The Intervals]] tournament
- Maintained human disguise during competition
- True nature as Couatl was eventually revealed or discovered
