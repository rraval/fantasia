The Piano Man (also called the Man of Strings) is the frail but powerful ruler who overthrew [[Belle]] and established the age of order and reason. He resides in the floating fortress of [[Tensegrity]].

## Description

- Physically frail old man, barely alive
- Eyes blaze with life
- Able to quickly perceive intimate details about people
- Requires [[Tonic]] (blue luminescent potion) to maintain his power
- Sits in front of a giant piano

## Abilities

- Can tap into the rhythms of the universe when powered by [[Tonic]]
- Extremely perceptive - sees through tricks and knows people's histories
- Powerful enough to force prophecies from seers
- Has a presence in every plane through [[Tensegrity]]

## History

- United the seven nation army to organize resistance against [[Belle]]
- Eradicated [[Belle]]'s influence from all 7 [[Planar System|planes]] of existence
- Brought about the "age of reason" with diplomacy and cooperation
- Established [[Tensegrity]], a singular stronghold with presence in every plane
- Reign is now crumbling and ending

## Relationships

- Served by [[Amras]] and [[Uptown Girl]]
- Funded in his early days by [[Elyas]]
- Now opposed by [[Elyas]], [[Amras]], and [[Uptown Girl]] in a coup attempt
- Guarded by forces loyal to him
- Party competed in [[The Intervals]] under his banner

## Chronology

### Meeting the Party

- Party brought [[Augur of Melodies]] to him at [[Tensegrity]]
- [[Amras]] brought him [[Tonic]] at the elevator
- [[Uptown Girl]] escorted party and Amras up to his chamber
- Thanked party for saving the Augur, dismissing [[The Synth]] as an annoyance
- Drank the [[Tonic]], undergoing physical transformation
- Started playing piano and tapping into [[Forces|universal rhythms]]
- Began singing [[Piano Man|"Piano Man"]]: "Son can you play me a memory? I'm not really sure how it goes..."
- Forced the prophecy from the Augur through musical duet
- Augur proclaimed: "The answer, my friend, is blowin' in the wind" and collapsed dead
- Prophecy revealed god of winds is due - "it can't be worse than the tambourine man"
- After exertion, reverted to sickly thin old self
- Knew party had saved [[Lyric]] and dealt with [[Queen of Hearts]]
- Granted party honor of competing under his banner in [[The Intervals]] in exchange for saving Augur

### Self-Sacrifice

- Stabbed himself with the [[Knife of Transposition]] to foil [[The Coup]]
- His soul was captured in the knife's gem
- Intentional act of defiance against [[Uptown Girl]] and [[Amras]]
- Denied the conspirators their prize by removing himself from play

### Dream Sequence

- Party entered Orchestral Plane (dream plane) which appears as an orchestra
- Met half of Piano Man
- Showed the dagger to strings section
