Psycho Killer is a hooded figure who was capturing souls in gems at a night market before being stopped by the party.

## Description

- Hooded figure hiding their identity
- Captured souls using ruby gems
- Associated with ritual magic

## Abilities

- Soul capture - could draw essence/soul from victims into gems
- The light emanating from victims was sucked into the ruby
- Working toward completing a 12-gem ritual

## Chronology

### Night Market

- Captured a lady at the market
- Light emanated from her body, being sucked into a ruby
- Victim said "Fa-fa-fa-fa" and dropped to the floor
- Chase ensued through buildings
- Went down into a basement
- Was being chased by party
- Got incinerated during pursuit
- Left behind pocket book and the ruby

### Basement Discovery
- Ruby had trace mounting marks (designed to be mounted on something)
- Was taking it to room with 12 other gem mounts
- Appeared to be preparing some sort of 12-gem ritual

### Investigation
- Thugs revealed they were hired help
- Only saw [[Father Banes]] (priest of town) come into basement besides the Hood
- Connection to Father Banes suggests religious or cult involvement

### Disruption
- [[Buddington]] started grabbing random gems from ritual
- This triggered an earthquake
- Police showed up and arrested everybody
- Ritual was stopped before completion

## Connections

- Connected to [[Father Banes]], priest of the town
- Hired thugs to guard operation
- Working on 12-gem soul ritual
