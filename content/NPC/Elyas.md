Elyas is an elven leader who rules from an elf kingdom. He initially fueled [[Piano Man]]'s rise to power but became disillusioned as the reign crumbled, ultimately funding a coup against him.

## Description

- Elven leader with significant resources
- Rules from an unknown stronghold
- Has become disillusioned with [[Piano Man]]'s crumbling reign
- Brother of [[Vadu]]

## Background

- Originally funded [[Piano Man|Man of Strings]] in the rebellion against [[Belle]]
- Became disillusioned as Piano Man's reign deteriorated
- Secretly funded [[Uptown Girl]] and [[Amras]] in coup attempt

## Relationships

- Originally supported [[Piano Man]]
- Now opposes Piano Man through [[Uptown Girl]] and [[Amras]]

## Chronology

### Revelations

- Revealed to have funded [[The Coup]] via [[Uptown Girl]] and [[Amras]]
- Became disillusioned after [[Piano Man]]'s reign started crumbling
- [[The Coup|Coup attempt]] failed when Piano Man stabbed himself with [[Knife of Transposition]]
- Piano Man intentionally trapped his own soul, denying Elyas and his conspirators their prize
- Party impersonated/pretended to be Elyas at some point
- Guards were confused, saying "there is no Elyas here" - [[Uptown Girl]] and [[Amras]] were denying his involvement
- [[Vadu]] (Elyas's brother) had "definitely perished" during the coup events
