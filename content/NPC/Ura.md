Ura is the Arm of Law who worked alongside [[Vadu]], and later attempted to lead [[Lyric]] after his death, facing opposition from [[The Leader]].

## Role

- Arm of Law in first judicial office of [[Lyric]]
- Executed sentences after [[Vadu]] determined guilt
- Attempted to lead Lyric after [[Vadu]]'s death and the [[Material Plane Riots]]
- Opposed by [[The Leader]] for control of Lyric

## Abilities

- Powerful combatant capable of beheading in combat
- Authority to carry out legal sentences
- Leadership capabilities to organize survivors

## Chronology

### Party's Trial

- Present at party's trial as Arm of Law
- Announced judgments for each accused
- Sentenced [[Darrow]] to duel for his freedom
- Beheaded Darrow, splattering blood on "LAW ONE" sign
- [[The Leader]] and minion fought Ura trying to reach [[Vadu]]
- Party escaped during the chaos of The Leader's interruption

### Post-Riots Leadership

- [[Vadu]] was killed at [[Queen of Hearts]]' ball in the Feywild
- Ura attempted to lead the people of Lyric after riots and Vadu's death
- Found by guards of Town of Diamonds trying to coordinate evacuation
- Asked about [[Vadu]]
- Party told her Vadu was dead
- Hung her head in shame
- Party helped evacuate ~1500 people from Lyric to the material plane

### Conflict with The Leader

- Knocked on door of [[Tinkers Guild]] where party was resting
- Explained she was trying to lead the people of Lyric
- Someone styling themselves as "[[The Leader]]" tried to seize interim control
- The Leader was taking her presence poorly
- **Major Crisis: The water supply was poisoned**
- The Leader had seized the only antidote
- The Leader retreated into the Duke's mansion and set off numerous traps
- Ura had already lost 12 followers ("red shirts") trying to storm the mansion
- Asked the party for help dealing with The Leader
- Party infiltrated the Duke's mansion and killed The Leader
- Presumably Ura was able to recover the antidote and secure her leadership
- Ura's leadership of Lyric was secured after The Leader's death

## Connections

- Worked with [[Vadu]] (Head of Law) - who died at Queen of Hearts' ball
- Opposed by [[The Leader]] for control of Lyric
- Allied with the party to defeat The Leader
- Attempted to lead survivors of [[Lyric]] after [[Material Plane Riots]]
