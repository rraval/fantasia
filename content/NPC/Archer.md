Archer is the captain of [[Queen of Hearts]]' guard, a skilled military leader who helped the party during their time in the Feywild.

## Description

- Captain of Queen of Hearts' guard
- Commands troops and scouts
- Skilled military tactician

## Abilities

- Leadership and military command
- Skilled archer (implied by name)
- Can organize and coordinate large forces

## Relationships

- Loyal to [[Queen of Hearts]]
- Worked with the party to help transport humans
- Separated from Queen during the ball massacre
- Provided intelligence and support to party

## Chronology

### Dress of Love Heist

- Led troop pursuing party at Princess of Diamonds' mansion
- Party created distraction causing Archer to split majority of troops to investigate
- Continued toward mansion with only two scouts
- Scouts triggered party's traps but took minimal damage
- Realized she'd been tricked and backed away to get troops
- Two scouts were killed by party (one looked at [[Helia Oriela]] wearing [[Dress of Love]] and saw [[Queen of Hearts]])

### The Ball

- Had been separated from [[Queen of Hearts]] during the ball
- Pre-arranged for party to enter with forged menagerie invitation
- Guards had been told to "obviously let party pass through" via Archer's orders
- Hadn't been seen in a while during the ball events

### Portal Assistance

- Present when Queen woke after the ball massacre
- Participated in information exchange with party and Queen
- Revealed the eyeballs in potions matched a cyclops skull (clairvoyance potions)
- Sent to fetch an evil person for soul harvesting demonstration
- Fetched Al, the barkeeper from "The Roly Poly Bat Faced Girl"

### Hunting Red Caps

- Learned of 5 red caps in frenzy near hilly region
- Took wagon with party to hunt the red caps
- Participated in red cap trap at rope bridge
- Was stranded on far side of ravine after bridge was cut
- Took couple hours to walk around ravine to rejoin party
